# What changed: 2026-11-17

One new page compares three agent payment protocols, using only source cards the wiki already cites. Later the same day, headings, openings and page descriptions were reworded; no facts, citations or dates changed.

- **New comparison.** [AP2 vs ACP vs x402: which AI agent payment protocol does what?](../analyses/ap2-vs-acp-vs-x402.md) puts the three open protocols in one table (best for, cost, strength, limitation), then answers common questions about each. It is the first page in [Analyses](../analyses/README.md), which now appears in navigation.
- **Glossary.** New entries: [A2A](../glossary.md#a2a), [facilitator](../glossary.md#facilitator) and [merchant of record](../glossary.md#merchant-of-record).
- **Links.** [Agent payments](../rails/agent-payments.md) and [Payments](../payments/README.md) link to the new comparison.
- **Updated date.** Every page now shows "Updated" with its date, where it used to say "Last checked". The date is the same. Source cards still show the date they were retrieved.
- **Question headings.** Section headings on the five broker pages, the rails pages and the approval-model pages are now questions, such as "Can an AI agent trade at Robinhood?" and "Which brokers ask you to confirm each order?".
- **Answer first.** Broker pages open with a direct answer; the note on source cards and "unknown" moved to the end of each broker page. The FAQ, Trading, launch-broker comparison, advisor, connection and gaps pages open with an answer instead of a description of the page. The Trading page no longer repeats its date under the first question.
- **Page descriptions.** Fifteen page summaries, also used as search descriptions, were shortened or lengthened to 120–160 characters without changing their facts.

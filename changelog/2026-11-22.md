# What changed: 2026-11-22

- **Link agent spend.** [Link wallet for agents](../payments/link-wallet-for-agents.md#how-much-are-agents-spending) now has figures from a 2026-11-20 post and article by Stripe's chief economist on his personal X account (not an official Stripe release): daily spend on Link Agent Wallet was nearly 80-fold higher in mid-November than at the start of September, as a [seven-day rolling average](../glossary.md#seven-day-rolling-average), and spend doubled in the last month. Last week, over 40% of that volume was on airfare, travel, and lodging, roughly a third on retail, and a quarter on other categories. More than half of consumers who make one agentic purchase come back for a second, typically in the same category. Dollar amounts and user counts are unknown. The figures are for Link agent spend as a whole. [Grok Bot](../personal-money/grok-bot.md), [Instinct](../personal-money/instinct.md) and [Meta Muse](../personal-money/meta-muse.md) each have a cited line, and the glossary defines the rolling average.
- **Link spend visuals.** [Link wallet for agents](../payments/link-wallet-for-agents.md) now has a category-split bar of those mid-November shares, and a diagram of how a purchase moves from the person to the agent, to Link, to the merchant. On a phone, the spend figures table wraps instead of scrolling sideways.

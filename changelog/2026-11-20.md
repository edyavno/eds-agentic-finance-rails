# What changed: 2026-11-20

Ten items were added, with 23 new source cards. Three new pages cover AWS's AgentCore payments, Lightspark Agent Accounts and institutional markets.

- **Citi and Chase terms.** [Banks and outside agents](../personal-money/banks-and-outside-agents.md#what-do-the-citi-and-chase-user-agreements-say) now quotes the banks' own user agreements. Citi's new Section H and Chase's Digital Services Agreement (both marked November 2026; the exact days are unknown) say that if you give an outside AI agent your login, the bank treats its actions as authorized by you. TechCrunch's report adds Bank of America, Citizens and PNC, whose own wording the wiki has not seen. The [overview](../overview.md) and [Mandates and data access](../rails/mandates-and-data-access.md#what-have-a-regulator-and-two-banks-said-about-agent-authority) link to it.
- **Singapore's regulator.** The Monetary Authority of Singapore said on 2026-11-19 that it would not advise giving personal agents login credentials, and that it will facilitate a [Know Your Agent](../rails/agent-identity.md#what-is-know-your-agent) protocol and an agentic money sandbox. The protocol's text and date are unknown.
- **AWS.** New page [Amazon Bedrock AgentCore payments](../payments/agentcore-payments.md): agents pay over x402 and MPP within a session budget, and since 2026-11-19 can pay AWS Marketplace sellers in US dollars on the AWS bill. The Region list and pricing are unknown. [x402](../payments/x402.md) links to it.
- **Lightspark.** New page [Lightspark Agent Accounts](../payments/lightspark-agent-accounts.md), launched 2026-11-17: an agent holds and spends money within limits the owner sets and pays Visa merchants with a card valid for one purchase. Price and consumer countries are unknown.
- **Mastercard and Ant International.** [Mastercard Agent Pay](../payments/mastercard-agent-pay.md#what-did-the-ant-international-coffee-purchase-show) covers the live coffee purchase on Agent Pay with Verifiable Intent and the Agent Pay for Machines pilot in Singapore. Mastercard says Agent Pay for Machines was introduced in June, while its own product page is dated 2026-10-20; the two are not reconciled. The purchase's date, amount, merchant and country are unknown.
- **AXS.** [Agent payments](../rails/agent-payments.md#can-an-agent-pay-bills) covers AXS opening its Singapore bill payment network to agents through an MCP server, with Visa and Mastercard showing demonstrations at the Singapore FinTech Festival. [AP2](../payments/ap2.md), [Visa](../payments/visa-intelligent-commerce.md) and [Mastercard](../payments/mastercard-agent-pay.md) pages link to it.
- **Coinbase Advisor.** [Coinbase](../providers/coinbase.md#how-is-coinbase-advisor-different-from-an-outside-agent) now explains how Coinbase's own AI advisor differs from outside agents that connect to your account: trading only with your explicit permission, Coinbase One members in the US.
- **Visa in Europe.** [Visa Intelligent Commerce](../payments/visa-intelligent-commerce.md#agentic-ready-in-europe) adds the 2026-11-18 Europe expansion (more than 85 acceptance participants and 40+ European issuers) and Visa's corrected global release of 2026-11-19, which adds J.P. Morgan Payments. [PayPal](../payments/paypal.md) and [Stripe](../payments/stripe.md) now cite the corrected release.
- **NETS and Ant International.** [Agent payments](../rails/agent-payments.md#is-there-an-agent-payment-protocol-for-mobile-wallets) covers an agreement to explore joining the Agentic Mobile Protocol, signed 2026-11-19. It is not a launch.
- **Sardine.** [Merchants and outside agents](../payments/merchants-and-outside-agents.md#what-does-sardine-say-about-agent-checkouts) describes Sardine's Know Your Human, all as Sardine's own claims.
- **Replit.** [Link wallet for agents](../payments/link-wallet-for-agents.md#how-does-replit-agent-buy-with-link): Replit's Agent waits for the owner to approve the final total before charging. US only, Core and Pro users, one-time purchases.
- **BNY and Microsoft.** New page [Institutional markets](../rails/institutional-markets.md) covers a pilot Treasury bill negotiation using BNY's Agentic Negotiation Protocol. Humans approved the final terms and the agents cannot execute. The trade's tenor, size, price and date are unknown. [Draft, then you submit](../approval-models/draft-then-submit.md#does-draft-then-submit-exist-outside-brokers) and [Agent identity](../rails/agent-identity.md#how-do-agents-from-different-firms-identify-each-other) link to it.
- **Glossary and gaps.** New glossary entries: [Agentic Mobile Protocol (AMP)](../glossary.md#agentic-mobile-protocol-amp), [Agentic Negotiation Protocol (ANP)](../glossary.md#agentic-negotiation-protocol-anp), [Know Your Agent (KYA)](../glossary.md#know-your-agent-kya), [Know Your Human (KYH)](../glossary.md#know-your-human-kyh), [MPP](../glossary.md#mpp) and [SAFR](../glossary.md#safr). [Gaps](../gaps.md) lists what is still unknown.

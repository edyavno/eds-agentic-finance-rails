# What changed: 2026-11-19

Two items were added: news reports on Chase and agent card payments, and OpenAI's steps for adding a Chase card to ChatGPT Wallet.

- **Chase and card numbers.** [Banks and outside agents](../personal-money/banks-and-outside-agents.md#what-about-shopping-with-a-card) now covers a Wall Street Journal report of 2026-11-13 that JPMorgan Chase blocks agents when consumers give the bots their card information. The same report says cards from Bank of America, Capital One and Citigroup are being used by agents when consumers enable them. Both are third-party sources.
- **Link and virtual cards.** [Link wallet for agents](../payments/link-wallet-for-agents.md#does-link-work-with-chase-cards) describes the one-time virtual card Stripe uses for Chase cards. [Merchants and outside agents](../payments/merchants-and-outside-agents.md#what-do-merchants-say-about-agent-virtual-cards) adds what merchants say about its cost.
- **Chase cards in ChatGPT Wallet.** [ChatGPT Wallet](../payments/chatgpt-wallet.md#how-do-i-add-a-chase-card) lists the steps to add a Chase card: approve the connection in the Chase mobile app, as the primary cardholder.
- **Glossary.** New entry: [Agentic token](../glossary.md#agentic-token).

# What changed: 2026-11-15

No facts were added or removed, and no source cards changed.

- **Launch.** The wiki is now public at https://agenticfinancerails.com. The old alias domains redirect there.
- **Front page.** Now a short version of the [README](../README.md): the headline, one line on what the wiki covers, five "What we cover" cards with live counts, a "Start here" row, a small box for AI agents, and a footer with the disclaimer, license, corrections link and a link here.
- **Trading short answer.** "Which brokers let AI agents trade, and who approves the order?" and its five-broker answer now open the [Trading](../directory.md) page, with the same citations as the [launch-broker comparison](../comparisons/launch-brokers.md).
- **Section tables.** [Payments](../payments/README.md) and [Personal money agents](../personal-money/README.md) each gained a generated table of what an agent can do, what you control and whether there is an official MCP server.
- **What changed.** This folder now has a generated home page, [What changed](README.md), with the wiki in numbers (the stats that used to sit on the front page) and every update, newest first.
- **Navigation.** Breadcrumbs now point to real section pages. Every page opens with its one-line summary and a visible "Last checked" date. The [rails](../rails/README.md) page links the [approval models](../approval-models/README.md).
- **Glossary.** New entries: [approval model](../glossary.md#approval-model), [human in the loop](../glossary.md#human-in-the-loop) and [rails](../glossary.md#rails).
- **For search engines and agents.** Breadcrumb, dataset and FAQ structured data, plus page types and modified dates.
- **Trading page.** The Directory is now called [Trading](../directory.md) (same address) and covers brokers only. Payment programs, personal money agents and advisor tools are on their own section pages; the Payments and Personal money tables gained the operator and availability columns that used to be on the directory.
- **FAQ.** New [FAQ](../faq.md) page: six short answers, each footnoted from sources already cited elsewhere in the wiki.
- **Answer first.** Pages that opened with a table or a date now open with a plain-English answer line: [agent identity](../rails/agent-identity.md), [agent payments](../rails/agent-payments.md), [mandates and data access](../rails/mandates-and-data-access.md), [human in the loop](../build/human-in-the-loop.md) and the three [advisor](../advisors/README.md) pages.
- **Less clutter.** Pages show only the "Last checked" date, not a build date or reading time. The sitemap uses each page's last-checked date. The empty Analyses section is hidden from navigation until it has pages.
- **Footer wording.** The disclaimer now reads simply "Not investment advice."
- **Guide for agents.** [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) is now a short reader's guide: where to start, how citations work, when to say "unknown", and how to send corrections, including what is recorded with a note.
- **Wording.** The [overview](../overview.md) now introduces Public as "Not yet profiled here", the update history on the five launch-broker pages now opens with "First version of this page.", and notes about our own source set now say "our sources", "sources we cite" or "not yet reviewed here". Facts, dates and citations are unchanged.

# What changed: 2026-11-18

Ten announcements from the past week were added, each from the company's own page. Five new pages cover Gemini, Starling Assistant and Wealth.com, and new sections cover the rest.

- **Coinbase.** [Coinbase](../providers/coinbase.md) now lists its Agent Controls: a maximum trade size, a 24-hour volume limit and an auto-stop after a set percentage loss, all enforced by Coinbase. Product permissions are still marked "coming soon". Coinbase's official ChatGPT plugin shows confirmation cards for placing, editing and cancelling orders, while the custom MCP connection trades without per-trade approval. The [launch brokers comparison](../comparisons/launch-brokers.md) and the [per-order confirm](../approval-models/per-order-confirm.md) and [separate account](../approval-models/separate-account.md) pages say the same.
- **Public.** Release 0.11.0 of Public's self-hosted MCP server enforces a preflight and user confirmation on every order, with optional quantity and value limits. It is covered on the [overview](../overview.md), [local MCP servers and CLIs](../rails/local-mcp-and-cli.md) and [per-order confirm](../approval-models/per-order-confirm.md). Whether Public's hosted server has it is unknown.
- **Visa.** [Visa Intelligent Commerce](../payments/visa-intelligent-commerce.md) explains Agentic Ready, which Visa opened to merchants, payment providers and acquirers on 2026-11-17 with more than 140 participants. [Stripe](../payments/stripe.md) and [PayPal](../payments/paypal.md) note their part.
- **Mastercard.** [Mastercard Agent Pay](../payments/mastercard-agent-pay.md) describes a live flight purchase by an AI agent, announced 2026-11-12, that used Verifiable Intent. The airline, date and volume are unknown.
- **Plaid.** [Plaid](../personal-money/plaid.md), [Banks and outside agents](../personal-money/banks-and-outside-agents.md) and [Agent identity](../rails/agent-identity.md) cover Agent Detection, launched 2026-11-16.
- **New page: Gemini.** [Gemini with Google Wallet](../personal-money/gemini-google-wallet.md): US Gemini users can connect Wallet and Plaid-linked accounts for read-only money answers.
- **New page: Starling.** [Starling Assistant](../personal-money/starling-assistant.md): a UK bank's assistant that will set up payments from voice or text, with a tap to approve. Announced, not yet available to everyone.
- **New page: Wealth.com.** [Wealth.com](../advisors/wealth-com.md): an estate and tax connector now in ChatGPT, and an assistant inside Schwab Advisor Center.
- **Merchants.** [Merchants and outside agents](../payments/merchants-and-outside-agents.md) adds Target, which allows only a list of approved agents, and Booking.com, which bars AI-powered assistants without written permission.
- **Glossary.** New entries: [Agent Detection](../glossary.md#agent-detection), [Agentic Ready](../glossary.md#agentic-ready), [Elicitation](../glossary.md#elicitation) and [Verifiable Intent](../glossary.md#verifiable-intent).
- **Section pages.** [Providers](../providers/README.md), [Sources](../sources/README.md) and [Comparisons](../comparisons/README.md) now have their own pages. Before this, those three folder links in the left-hand menu led to a "not found" page.
- **Gaps.** [Gaps](../gaps.md) lists what is still unknown, such as whether Coinbase's auto-stop cancels open orders.

# What changed: 2026-11-21

- **Starter prompts.** [Starter prompts](../build/starter-prompts.md) now starts with research questions this wiki can answer, then prompts for coding agents. Each section has a "start every session" preamble. The prompts use the live site URLs, so you can paste them without cloning the repo, and the copy button stays visible on a phone.
- **Front page.** The Start here row now includes "Try a starter prompt", which opens [Starter prompts](../build/starter-prompts.md).
- **Coinbase for Agents usage.** [Coinbase](../providers/coinbase.md#which-ai-apps-trade-the-most-on-coinbase) now has a table of which AI apps trade the most, from Coinbase's official X post of 2026-11-21 and the video attached to it: Grok Bot 40.4% of notional trading volume for 11/09 - 11/15 (2026 inferred). Dollar totals are unknown. [Grok Bot](../personal-money/grok-bot.md) has a line on it, and the glossary has two new terms, [LLM client](../glossary.md#llm-client) and [notional volume](../glossary.md#notional-volume).
- **Cheatsheets.** [Approval models](../approval-models/README.md#who-says-yes-and-who-enforces-it) now has a two-axis visual of who says yes and who enforces it for the five launch brokers. [Payments](../payments/README.md#which-controls-does-each-program-use) has a control matrix, [Personal money agents](../personal-money/README.md#read-pay-or-move-money) has a read / pay / move grid, and [x402](../payments/x402.md#x402-request-flow) has a request-flow sequence. Each one has a table with sources. No facts were removed.

# What changed: 2026-11-25

- **Walmart Agent Gateway and Muse.** From 2026-11-24, [Muse](../personal-money/meta-muse.md#where-can-muse-shop) can shop Walmart through the [Walmart Agent Gateway](../glossary.md#agent-gateway-walmart). Shoppers set an [Agent Profile](../glossary.md#agent-profile) in their Walmart account, including a spending limit and purchase approvals. Which other agents can use the gateway, and the default spending-limit figures, are unknown.
- **Gopuff and JD Sports.** Muse can shop Gopuff once you approve the order. JD Sports is a UK launch connector partner; Muse launched in the UK that day for people aged 18 or over, on iOS, Android and muse.ai.
- **Cashfree Wallet.** A new [Cashfree Wallet](../payments/cashfree-wallet.md) page covers a prepaid wallet in India, in invite-only beta, whose AgentPay [MCP](../glossary.md#mcp) server pays everyday bills through [BBPS](../glossary.md#bbps). Fees, the launch date and numeric limits are unknown.
- **FCA agentic AI pages.** The UK's Financial Conduct Authority published firm and consumer pages on 2026-11-24, now on [mandates and data access](../rails/mandates-and-data-access.md#what-have-a-regulator-and-two-banks-said-about-agent-authority) and [banks and outside agents](../personal-money/banks-and-outside-agents.md). A linked DRCF joint statement PDF was not opened.
- **PayPal Agentic VC.** [PayPal](../payments/paypal.md#agentic-vc-merchant-virtual-card-terms) merchant terms, last updated 2026-11-23, describe settlement by a one-time [Agentic VC](../glossary.md#agentic-vc). Which agent services trigger it is unknown.
- **Google AI Mode and Wallet.** From 2026-11-23, [Gemini with Google Wallet](../personal-money/gemini-google-wallet.md#what-about-search-ai-mode) notes that Search AI Mode can opt in to connect Wallet. It is read-only. The countries for that rollout are unknown.
- **Akamai and Visa TAP.** [Agent identity](../rails/agent-identity.md) records Akamai's own blog claims that it verifies Visa TAP signatures at the edge. This is not a Visa source.

# What changed: 2026-11-27

- **Tasks in Gemini.** A new [Tasks in Gemini](../personal-money/gemini-tasks.md) page covers a mode in Google's Gemini app that can carry out tasks, including shopping and checkout with Google Pay. Google says it uses [AP2](../glossary.md#ap2) for purchases. You can set a hard spending cap, and Gemini is designed to ask for confirmation or pre-approval before clicking "Buy," unless you set a routine action to "Always allow." Default cap amounts, which merchants it can check out with, and whether checkout works outside the United States are unknown. [Gemini with Google Wallet](../personal-money/gemini-google-wallet.md) remains a read-only insights connection.
- **Holiday traffic.** Cloudflare Radar's Black Friday / Cyber Monday 2026 Spotlight and Forter's Agentic BFCM page both report personal-agent shopping figures that change over time. The numbers on this wiki were read in the early afternoon ET on 27 November 2026. Forter's figures are its own vendor-reported numbers from its merchant network. See [Merchants and outside agents](../payments/merchants-and-outside-agents.md#what-do-holiday-traffic-reports-say-about-personal-agents).
- **paymove.** A new [paymove](../payments/paymove.md) page covers agent payments in Poland with [BLIK](../glossary.md#blik). Each payment is confirmed in the customer's banking app. An x402 prepaid-balance mode exists but is switched off.
- **GoCardless.** A new [GoCardless agentic payments](../payments/gocardless.md) page covers a hosted MCP server that can set up a Direct Debit mandate or a one-off bank payment, with a secure form and final approval. The product is in early access in the UK and was built in the FCA's Live AI Testing programme.
- **LianLian XCard.** A Lianlian DigiTech press release says this business card, which lets authorised AI agents pay within set limits, is powered by Visa Intelligent Commerce Connect. See [Visa Intelligent Commerce](../payments/visa-intelligent-commerce.md).
- **x402 Lightning.** The x402 Foundation published an `exact` scheme for Lightning Bitcoin (`lnbtc`). The scheme describes a machine-to-machine invoice and preimage flow; it does not describe a person approving a payment. See [x402](../payments/x402.md).

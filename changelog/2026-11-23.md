# What changed: 2026-11-23

- **Visa CLI.** A new [Visa CLI](../payments/visa-cli.md) page covers a Visa Crypto Labs product: after a US Visa card check, an agent gets a signing key, is listed in the [Visa Agent Directory](../glossary.md#visa-agent-directory), and can take paid tasks and pay HTTP 402 requests in [OUSD](../glossary.md#ousd) on [Tempo](../glossary.md#tempo), within limits the owner set. Access is by invitation. It is separate from [Visa Intelligent Commerce](../payments/visa-intelligent-commerce.md).
- **Klarna in Adyen Agentic checkout.** On [Payments](../payments/README.md#klarna-in-adyen-agentic-checkout), a 2026-11-20 announcement says merchants that already offer Klarna through Adyen can switch it on at agent checkout, with the shopper confirming every purchase. Klarna's payment-handler specification URL and the AI platforms are unknown.
- **Stripe Treasury and Issuing for agents.** On [Stripe](../payments/stripe.md), Treasury write access (transfers, currency conversion and vendor payments within limits) is in public preview for business accounts, and Issuing for agents is in private preview.
- **PayPal Agent Ready.** [PayPal](../payments/paypal.md#agent-ready) documents how Braintree and PayPal merchants can accept agent checkout on ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Gemini over ACP or UCP.
- **pay.sh.** The [x402](../payments/x402.md) and [agent payments](../rails/agent-payments.md) pages now include pay.sh, a client and gateway for x402 and MPP payments in stablecoins on Solana, launched 2026-05-05 by the Solana Foundation with Google Cloud.
- **Payment Choice Framework.** [Personal money agents](../personal-money/README.md#how-should-an-agent-choose-a-way-to-pay) records a Consumer Reports and Affirm draft that helps an assistant recommend how to pay. The GitHub repository says it is for review only, not for real-world use. In tests, pass rates were higher with the framework; the results do not establish improved consumer outcomes or readiness for consumer use.
- **Bank-account agents.** [Banks and outside agents](../personal-money/banks-and-outside-agents.md) and [Mandates and data access](../rails/mandates-and-data-access.md) quote Federal Reserve Governor Michael S. Barr on AI agents in retail accounts and the authentication, consent and capability controls he called for.
- **Webull AU region.** [Webull](../providers/webull.md) local OpenAPI MCP 1.2.8 enables the AU region. The hosted Cloud MCP regions are unchanged, and the AU disclaimer text is unknown.

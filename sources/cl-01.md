# Use Personal Finance in Claude (Claude Help Center)

**Official source** · Anthropic · support · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18185620-use-personal-finance-in-claude)

## Summary

Claude's help article for Personal Finance. It is in beta on Pro, Max and Ultra plans in the US only, on the Claude web, desktop, iOS and Android apps, and not on Free, Team or Enterprise plans; rollout is gradual. People link banks through Plaid (up to 16 banks). For shared accounts Claude can read names, types, last four digits and up to 24 months of transactions; for investments, loans and cards it reads balances and credit limits. Access is read-only. Claude reads linked accounts without asking each time unless the person changes the Plaid connector setting, and asks before linking or unlinking a bank. People can unlink a bank or disconnect Claude at my.plaid.com. Personal Finance is not available in Claude Duet.

## Short quotes

> Personal Finance can only read your accounts. It can’t move, manage, or invest your money.

> This feature is in beta on Pro, Max, and Ultra plans in the US only.

> Personal Finance isn’t available in Claude Duet.

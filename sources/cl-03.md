# Claude Duet safety, privacy and security

**Official source** · Anthropic · official-doc · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-duet-safety-privacy-and-security)

## Summary

Anthropic's safety page for Claude Duet. Before it sends a sensitive message, pays or deletes, Duet shows the action, destination and content and waits for approval, and a separate automated check runs alongside. Some actions stay off-limits even with approval, such as trading securities and banking actions like moving money between accounts; paying or sending money cannot be covered by a don't-ask-again rule. For purchases Duet pays with a one-time card from a wallet like Link from Stripe, limited to the merchant and amount, and the person approves in the Link app. Duet never sees passwords or sign-in tokens.

## Short quotes

> Some actions stay off-limits even with your approval, like trading securities, banking actions such as moving money between accounts, and accessing someone else's account.

> For purchases, Claude Duet pays with a one-time card from a wallet like Link from Stripe, limited to that merchant and amount.

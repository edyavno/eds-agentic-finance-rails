# Connect Google Wallet to Gemini for personal finance insights

**Official source** · Google · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/google-wallet-gemini-app/)

## Summary

Google blog post, 2026-11-13. All Gemini app users in the U.S. can connect Google Wallet to Gemini, through Personal Intelligence settings, for answers informed by their spending, such as spending charts, forgotten subscriptions and budgets. Users can also link credit card and bank accounts to Wallet through Plaid. Google says Gemini can only read information from the accounts and cannot move, manage or invest money, and that users choose whether to connect and can turn the connection on or off at any time. It is rolling out on web and mobile (Android and iOS).

## Short quotes

> Rolling out today for all Gemini app users in the U.S., you can connect Google Wallet for responses informed by your spending habits.

> You also have the option to securely link your financial accounts from your credit cards and banks to Wallet via Plaid to unlock deeper insights.

> Gemini can only read information from your accounts; it can’t move, manage, or invest your money.

> You decide if and when you want to opt in to this experience, and you can turn the connection on or off at any time.

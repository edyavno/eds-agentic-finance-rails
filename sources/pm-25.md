# Visa Expands Agentic Ready to Help Scale Trusted AI Commerce across Europe

**Official source** · Visa (Visa UK newsroom) · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-20 · [open the original](https://www.visa.co.uk/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases.3478924.html)

## Summary

Visa press release of 2026-11-18, published on Visa's UK newsroom. Visa expanded Agentic Ready to the acceptance ecosystem in Europe, launching with more than 85 acceptance participants including Adyen, Barclaycard Payments, Checkout.com, Global Payments, Nuvei, PayPal, Stripe and Worldline. Merchants, payment service providers and acquirers can join the 40+ European issuers already taking part. The framework supports agentic transactions on consumer and commercial cards.

## Short quotes

> This latest phase is launching with more than 85 acceptance participants across Europe, with additional financial institutions, merchants and enablers expected to join throughout 2027.

> Merchants, payment service providers and acquirers can now join the 40+ European issuers already participating in Agentic Ready.

> This framework supports agentic transactions on both consumer and commercial cards, laying a foundation for agentic B2B services and payments.

> Today’s expansion of Visa Agentic Ready builds on the initial launch earlier this year, which now supports more than 40 European issuers as they prepare for agentic commerce.

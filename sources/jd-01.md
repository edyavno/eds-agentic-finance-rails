# JD partners with Meta as it launches Muse in UK

**Official source** · JD Sports Fashion plc · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-25 · [open the original](https://www.jdplc.com/jd-partners-with-meta-as-it-launches-muse-in-uk/)

## Summary

JD Sports Fashion plc RNS announcement dated 24 November 2026. JD says it has partnered with Meta for the UK launch of Muse, and that it is one of a select group of UK launch connector partners across retail, travel and transport. Through the connector, customers will be able to discover, compare and purchase JD products in Muse. JD says users are in full control, with Muse checking before it spends, sends or shares. Muse is available to anyone aged 18 and over in the UK from that day on iOS, Android and muse.ai, and is coming to Meta's AI glasses in the months ahead. Other connector partners are not named.

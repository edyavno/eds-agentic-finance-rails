# November 2026 Wealth.com Updates

**Official source** · Wealth.com · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://www.wealth.com/resources/press/november-2026-wealth-com-updates/)

## Summary

Wealth.com's November 2026 product update for advisors. Its AI assistant Ester now runs inside Schwab Advisor Center for every user there, with no Wealth.com license needed; it is a separate, lighter tool that does not connect to a Wealth.com client's Vault or history. The estate and tax planning connector that reached Claude in September 2026 now also works in ChatGPT, through Wealth.com's MCP, for estate document analysis with citations, balance sheets, beneficiary designations, filed-return summaries and tax scenarios. It is enabled at the firm level, subject to compliance approval. The page describes planning and analysis only; it does not describe trading or moving money.

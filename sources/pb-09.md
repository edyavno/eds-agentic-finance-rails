# Instinct gets the full financial picture with Plaid

**Official source** · Plaid · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://plaid.com/blog/plaid-powers-instinct-ai-assistant/)

## Summary

Plaid blog post, 2026-11-18. Plaid says it powers read-only financial account connectivity for Instinct, a personal assistant that works through iMessage and WhatsApp, for US consumers. After an Instinct account exists, the assistant prompts the user to authenticate accounts through Plaid and then reads balances and transactions alongside other connections such as email and calendar. Plaid says Instinct asks for approval before taking next steps (for example drafting a dispute) and lists ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, Grok, Muse and Perplexity's Personal CFO as other platforms it connects. It gives company-reported figures (35% of users connecting a card within three weeks; 800+ AI-native startups building on Plaid).

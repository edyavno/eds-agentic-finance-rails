# 'Talk to transact': Starling customers can use their voice to send money

**Official source** · Starling Bank · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://www.starlingbank.com/news/-talk-to-transact-starling-customers-can-use-their-voice-to-send-money/)

## Summary

Starling Bank newsroom post, 2026-11-13. Starling is adding a payment capability to its in-app Starling Assistant, part of its Starling Intelligence agentic AI platform. Personal and joint account holders will be able to give voice or text prompts such as "Pay the plumber £200", and the assistant sets up the payment, which the customer authorises with a screen tap. It will reach all retail customers by the end of 2026; small business customers will "soon" get a similar feature for paying invoices from a photo. This is announced, not yet available to everyone. The post does not give payment limits.

## Short quotes

> Starling Bank is launching a new payment capability, powered by its Starling Intelligence agentic AI platform, that enables retail customers to set up payments quickly and easily simply by speaking to the app on their phone.

> The in-app Starling Assistant will then set up the payment, which the customer can authorise with a screen tap.

> This capability will be rolled out to all retail customers by the end of the year.

> Small business customers will soon get a similar feature too, enabling them to instruct the Starling Assistant to “Pay this invoice” while uploading an image of the bill to be settled.

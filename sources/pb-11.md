# ChatGPT personal finance experience, powered by Plaid

**Official source** · Plaid · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://plaid.com/blog/chatgpt-personal-finance-plaid/)

## Summary

Plaid blog post, 2026-05-15. Plaid says OpenAI introduced a preview of a personal finance experience in ChatGPT powered by Plaid, for Pro users in the US.

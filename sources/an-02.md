## Summary

The same Mastercard and Ant International announcement as card an-01, distributed on PR Newswire on 2026-11-19. It reports a live consumer transaction through Antom on Mastercard Agent Pay with Verifiable Intent, and a completed Agent Pay for Machines pilot with Antom in Singapore.

## Short quotes

> In the live transaction, an AI agent purchased a cup of coffee based on the consumer's pre-captured intent, with acceptance supported by Antom. The transaction ran on Mastercard Agent Pay with Verifiable Intent, creating a verifiable record of what the consumer had authorized the agent to do.

> Introduced in June this year, Mastercard Agent Pay for Machines enables businesses to credential their AI agents, define what those agents are authorized to purchase and spend and enforce those limits at the point of payment.

> Antom enabled the AI agent to successfully use AP4M's credentialing, policy controls and payment authorization capabilities to purchase digital services within parameters set by the business.

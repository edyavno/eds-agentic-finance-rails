# BNY and Microsoft Complete First Agentic Negotiation of a U.S. Treasury Trade

**Official source** · BNY · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-20 · [open the original](https://www.bny.com/corporate/global/en/about-us/newsroom/press-release/bny-and-microsoft-complete-first-agentic-negotiation-of-a-us-treasury-trade.html)

## Summary

BNY press release of 2026-11-17. BNY and Microsoft completed what BNY calls an industry-first pilot trade negotiation of a U.S. Treasury bill using AI agents, built on BNY's Agentic Negotiation Protocol (ANP, patent pending). The agents exchanged offers and counteroffers, then human traders from both firms reviewed and approved the proposed terms. The release says agents cannot execute the trade for either firm. BNY plans to keep testing ANP.

## Short quotes

> BNY (NYSE: BNY), a global financial services company, and Microsoft completed an industry-first pilot trade negotiation of a U.S. Treasury bill using AI agents.

> ANP verifies participants and allows them to connect securely, enabling the agents to exchange offers and counteroffers, and confirms a shared understanding of the proposed terms.

> Human traders retain authority over final decisions and actions. Agents cannot execute the trade on behalf of either firm and trades are booked through existing internal trading systems.

> BNY plans to continue testing ANP, with a focus on expanding beyond the initial bilateral use case and evaluating additional negotiated workflows.

# Trading with your agent | Robinhood

**Official source** · Robinhood · support · retrieved 2026-11-19 · [open the original](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/trading-with-your-agent/)

## Summary

Agents can place long equities, options and crypto orders (no multi-leg options). 'Trade approvals' toggle: on = agent proposes, you place; off = agent trades without confirmation. Crypto not in some states incl. NY. The 2026-11-19 re-read adds the Agent Settings path for MCP accounts (MCP account tab > Agent Settings > Safety Controls), crypto account prerequisites, the 45-day restoration rule after moving states, and tool tables that now also cover scanner, alerts, advanced-order and banking tools.

## Short quotes

> You can currently use a built-in or external agent to place long equities, options, and crypto orders.

> Trade approvals are turned on by default for Robinhood Agents (built-in agents), and are turned off by default for MCP accounts (external agents).

> Certain trades may still require your approval even when trade approvals are turned off.

> Crypto trading through your agent isn't available in some states, including New York.

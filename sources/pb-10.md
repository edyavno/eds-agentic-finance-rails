# Plaid powers financial insights in Claude

**Official source** · Plaid · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://plaid.com/blog/plaid-anthropic-financial-insights-in-claude/)

## Summary

Plaid blog post, 2026-11-05. Plaid says it powers account connections for Personal Finance, a new feature in Claude, for US consumers, and that Claude's access to connected accounts is read-only so it cannot move money. A user opens the Personal Finance tab, Claude prompts them to authenticate through Plaid, and Plaid shares the information needed, organizes raw transaction data and refreshes it when needed. Plaid says (company-reported) that nearly two-thirds of US people who connect bank accounts to AI assistants through Plaid had never used a Plaid-powered personal finance app.

## Short quotes

> Claude’s access to connected accounts is read-only, so it can’t move money.

> Plaid will share the necessary information needed to power the experience, organizing raw transaction data and refreshing the data when needed.

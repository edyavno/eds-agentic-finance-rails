# AXS Pioneers Agentic Bill Payments in South-East Asia, Opening Its Bill Payment Network to AI-Powered Partners

**Official source** · AXS · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-20 · [open the original](https://www.axs.com.sg/axs-enables-agentic-bill-payments-opening-its-bill-payment-network-to-ai-powered-partners/)

## Summary

AXS press release of 2026-11-18. AXS, a Singapore payment solutions provider, opens its bill payment network to AI agents through an MCP server. Visa and Mastercard are working with AXS to bring agentic bill payments into their ecosystems, and a pilot is under way with the Grab AI Assistant. The release says the solution is designed around MCP, the Universal Commerce Protocol, AP2 and MAS's Safeguards for Agentic Finance at Runtime (SAFR).

## Short quotes

> Through its Model Context Protocol (MCP) server, AXS is bringing together leading payment networks, banks and digital platforms to enable AI-powered bill payment experiences.

> Through a single integration, partners can deliver AI-driven bill payment experiences to their customers through AXS’s network of more than 800 billing organisations in Singapore, with access to billing services being progressively rolled out.

> Guides safeguards such as payment limits, user approvals and transaction accountability.

> Using an agentic token, the demonstration will show how personal agents could securely pay on a consumer’s behalf, with potential applications across other everyday payments and services.

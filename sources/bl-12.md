# AI bots are using credit cards. Banks are racing to keep up. (The Wall Street Journal, via Mint)

**Third-party source** · The Wall Street Journal (syndicated on Mint) · news · retrieved 2026-11-19 · [open the original](https://www.livemint.com/global/ai-bots-are-using-credit-cards-banks-are-racing-to-keep-up-11794532545442.html)

## Summary

Wall Street Journal report by AnnaMaria Andriotis, Ben Glickman and Gina Heeb, syndicated on Mint. Citing people familiar with the matter, it says JPMorgan Chase blocks agents when consumers give the bots their card information and has been slow to allow other solutions, including the Visa and Mastercard agentic tokens Stripe's Link uses. It says cards from Bank of America, Capital One and Citigroup are being used by agents when consumers enable them. For Chase cards and other issuers, Stripe's wallet uses one-time virtual cards equal to the purchase amount and then charges the consumer's actual card. Merchants say the virtual cards cost more to accept and make returns harder. The fingerprint covers the retained excerpt.

## Short quotes

> The bank has been blocking agents when consumers give the bots their card information and it has been slow to allow other solutions rival banks are trying, the people said.

> Cards from banks such as Bank of America, Capital One and Citigroup are being used by agents when consumers enable them.

> In those cases, Stripe’s wallet has been using one-time virtual cards that are equal to the purchase amount. Just after the transaction goes through, Stripe charges the purchase amount to the consumer’s actual card, which effectively pays off the virtual card.

> Merchants are complaining to Visa and Mastercard that they are incurring higher fees on average from the virtual card than they would be charged by the consumer’s actual card, according to people familiar with the matter.

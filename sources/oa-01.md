# Introducing ChatGPT Wallet

**Official source** · OpenAI · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-15 · [open the original](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet/)

## Summary

OpenAI announcement of ChatGPT Wallet, built in partnership with American Express, Mastercard and Visa. Users approve each purchase with a security check; the payment network then provides a payment token instead of the real card details. Initially for ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US through dots. Names fraud prevention partners (Forter, Riskified, Sardine, Signifyd) and payment service providers (Adyen, Checkout.com, Stripe), and asks merchants to recognize signed agent requests with Web Bot Auth.

## Short quotes

> Before each purchase, the user reviews the items, total, and selected card, then approves it with a security check such as Face ID or a fingerprint scan.

> After the user approves, the payment network provides a payment token—a substitute for the user’s actual card details—with a card number, security code, and expiration date.

> We’re also working with fraud prevention partners including Forter, Riskified, Sardine and Signifyd and payment service providers including Adyen, Checkout.com, and Stripe to help their systems recognize legitimate purchases made by agents.

> It will initially be available to ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 and older in the US with eligible US-issued cards.

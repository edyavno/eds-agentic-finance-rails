# Security and wallet control | AgentBank

**Official source** · AgentBank · official-doc · retrieved 2026-11-15 · [open the original](https://useagentbank.com/security)

## Summary

AgentBank security page: controls the owner sets (payment confirmation, spending limit on ChatGPT and Claude connections, approval threshold, recipient review, KYC, disconnect). Wallets use Privy infrastructure and AgentBank does not take custody. Operated by Thinking Horizon, Inc., which AgentBank says is registered with FinCEN as a Money Services Business.

## Short quotes

> AgentBank does not take custody of your wallet funds.

> Signing in does not approve every payment, and approving a payment does not automatically fund it.

> AgentBank is a financial technology product operated by Thinking Horizon, Inc., a Delaware corporation registered as a Money Services Business with the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network under registration number 31000341906306.

# User Agreement | eBay

**Official source** · eBay · terms · retrieved 2026-11-15 · [open the original](https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/member-behavior-policies/user-agreement?id=4259)

## Summary

eBay User Agreement, effective June 28, 2026 for existing users (previous amendment effective February 20, 2026). Users may not use automated means, including buy-for-me agents, LLM-driven bots or end-to-end flows that place orders without human review, without eBay's prior express permission.

> buy-for-me agents, LLM-driven bots, or any end-to-end flow that attempts to place orders without human review

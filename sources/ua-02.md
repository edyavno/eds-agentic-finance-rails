# Unauthorized third-party AI agents or assistants | United Airlines

**Official source** · United Airlines · support · retrieved 2026-11-15 · [open the original](https://www.united.com/en/us/fly/help-center/digital-trust-center/artificial-intelligence/third-party-ai-agents.html)

## Summary

United help page on unauthorized third-party AI agents: United restricts access by unauthorized agents directly and through travel agencies and aggregators; AI research and planning that does not touch united.com or the app is fine; agent bookings made before November 11, 2026 will not be canceled; assistive technology is not affected; United works with no third-party AI agents yet.

## Short quotes

> You can still use AI to research and plan trips, as long as the tool doesn't access united.com or the United app.

> agent bookings made prior to November 11, 2026 will not be canceled.

> Assistive technology for customers with disabilities isn't affected.

> Agencies can only use their own third-party AI tools with United's permission.

# Digital Services Agreement (J.P. Morgan Chase)

**Official source** · JPMorgan Chase · terms · retrieved 2026-11-20 · [open the original](https://static.chasecdn.com/content/dam/legal-agreements/library/en/dsa_la/versions/dsa_la.pdf)

## Summary

JPMorgan Chase's Digital Services Agreement, marked last updated November 2026. A notice says that, effective October 27, 2026, third parties including External AI, automated tools or data aggregators are prohibited from using a customer's Login Credentials, while approved third-party tools can reach account data through approved APIs with the customer's authorization. Section 9 says a customer who permits a third party, including External AI, to access their accounts is deemed to authorize its transactions and is responsible for resulting errors.

## Short quotes

> third parties — including External AI, automated tools, or data aggregators — are prohibited from using your Login Credentials to access the Digital Platforms or your accounts or Services thereon.

> We permit third-party applications and tools, including External AI, to access customer account data through approved application programming interfaces ("APIs") with your authorization.

> Expressly authorize that third party to transact in your account and the actions or transactions they initiate, request, or complete;

> SUBJECT TO APPLICABLE LAW, YOU ASSUME THE ENTIRE RISK FOR THE FRAUDULENT, UNAUTHORIZED OR OTHERWISE IMPROPER USE OF YOUR LOGIN CREDENTIALS.

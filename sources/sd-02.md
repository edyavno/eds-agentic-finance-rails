# Know Your Human (KYH) for Agentic Commerce | Sardine

**Official source** · Sardine · official-doc · retrieved 2026-11-20 · [open the original](https://www.sardine.ai/know-your-human)

## Summary

Sardine's product page for Know Your Human (KYH). It says KYH lets a merchant send the customer a verification link by SMS, and that the link expires after 5 minutes. It describes how an agent connects the customer's device to its session and passes session details to the merchant in HTTP headers. It says KYH is complementary to agentic commerce protocols and cautions that collecting a signal does not guarantee a successful dispute response.

## Short quotes

> Link expires 5 minutes after it’s created

> Agent-KYH-Provider and Agent-Context-Key identify the provider and session used to retrieve the customer’s device fingerprint and IP.

> Programs such as Visa Compelling Evidence 3.0 and Mastercard First-Party Trust have their own eligibility and evidence requirements; collecting a signal alone does not guarantee a successful dispute response.

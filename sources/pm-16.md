# Visa Expands Agentic Ready to Help Scale Trusted AI Commerce Globally

**Official source** · Visa · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://investor.visa.com/news/news-details/2026/Visa-Expands-Agentic-Ready-to-Help-Scale-Trusted-AI-Commerce-Globally/default.aspx)

## Summary

Visa press release, 2026-11-17. Visa expanded its Agentic Ready program, part of Visa Intelligent Commerce, from card issuers to the acceptance ecosystem: merchants, B2B suppliers, payment providers, acquirers and commerce platforms. It launches with more than 140 participants, including Adyen, Antom, Billtrust, Checkout.com, Fiserv, Global Payments, PayPal and Stripe, alongside 175+ issuers across 45 markets that were already taking part. Participants test how agent-initiated transactions (agentic network tokens) are recognized, authenticated and processed. Visa says more institutions, merchants and enablers are expected to join through 2027.

## Short quotes

> Merchants, B2B suppliers, payment providers, acquirers and commerce platforms can now join the 175+ issuers already participating in Agentic Ready across 45 markets.

> The program is launching with more than 140 participants, with additional financial institutions, merchants and enablers expected to join throughout 2027.

> PayPal is working with Visa to expand acceptance of Visa agentic tokens across its merchant network

> With Visa’s Agentic Ready program, we’re drawing on our work with agents and merchants to test ways to recognize agent-initiated payments

# A new personal finance experience in ChatGPT (OpenAI)

**Official source** · OpenAI · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://openai.com/index/personal-finance-chatgpt/)

## Summary

OpenAI's launch post, 2026-05-15, with updates dated 2026-06-25 (Plus and Pro in the US) and 2026-10-02 (rolling out to Free and Go users in the US). It says ChatGPT can access balances, transactions, investments and liabilities, cannot see full account numbers or make changes to accounts, and that connected accounts are linked through Plaid (support for more than 12,000 institutions, Intuit support coming). Disconnecting removes synced account data from OpenAI's systems within 30 days; conversation history is not affected.

## Short quotes

> It cannot see full account numbers or make any changes to your accounts.

> Once disconnected, your synced account data will be deleted from OpenAI’s systems within 30 days.

# Coinbase Advisor

**Official source** · Coinbase · official-doc · retrieved 2026-11-20 · [open the original](https://www.coinbase.com/advisor)

## Summary

Coinbase's product page for Coinbase Advisor, its AI-powered investment advisor inside the Coinbase app. The page says every recommendation is personalized and every action requires the user's approval, and the FAQ answers that the AI does not make trades on the user's behalf. During the Beta it is available to select users and to select Coinbase One subscribers at no additional cost. It lists the products the Beta supports and says prediction markets are being built.

## Short quotes

> As one of the world’s first SEC-registered AI-powered investment advisors, Advisor helps you turn market news, ideas, and opportunities into informed decisions.

> No. Think of Coinbase Advisor as an intelligent co-pilot: it analyzes the market, answers your questions, and builds complex strategies for you—but trading is only done with your explicit permission.

> Advisor works within the goals and risk preferences you set. Every recommendation is personalized, and every action requires your approval. Early access is available exclusively through Coinbase One.

> We are actively building support for prediction markets—which we intend to be available in upcoming releases.

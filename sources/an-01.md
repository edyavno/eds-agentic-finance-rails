# Mastercard and Ant International Advance Trusted Agentic Commerce in Asia Pacific (Ant International page)

**Official source** · Mastercard (posted by Ant International) · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-20 · [open the original](https://www.ant-intl.com/en/news/detail/mastercard-and-ant-international-advance-trusted-agentic-commerce-in-asia-pacific/)

## Summary

Mastercard announcement of 2026-11-19, posted on Ant International's site. It reports two milestones: a live consumer transaction through Antom, Ant International's merchant payment platform, and a completed Mastercard Agent Pay for Machines (AP4M) pilot with Antom in Singapore. In the live transaction an AI agent bought a cup of coffee from the consumer's pre-captured intent, on Mastercard Agent Pay with Verifiable Intent. The text says Agent Pay for Machines was introduced in June this year.

## Short quotes

> In the live transaction, an AI agent purchased a cup of coffee based on the consumer's pre-captured intent, with acceptance supported by Antom. The transaction ran on Mastercard Agent Pay with Verifiable Intent, creating a verifiable record of what the consumer had authorized the agent to do.

> Verifiable Intent is designed as an open, standards-based and protocol-agnostic trust layer that helps merchants and other participants establish trust in agent-initiated transactions by making consumer authorization verifiable.

> Introduced in June this year, Mastercard Agent Pay for Machines enables businesses to credential their AI agents, define what those agents are authorized to purchase and spend and enforce those limits at the point of payment.

> Antom enabled the AI agent to successfully use AP4M's credentialing, policy controls and payment authorization capabilities to purchase digital services within parameters set by the business.

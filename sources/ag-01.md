# Connect your AI agent to real-world money | AgentBank

**Official source** · AgentBank · official-doc · retrieved 2026-11-15 · [open the original](https://useagentbank.com/for-developers)

## Summary

AgentBank developer page: an MCP server (remote at https://mcp.useagentbank.com over OAuth 2.1 with PKCE, or a local stdio server) that lets an agent collect, hold, convert and send money to bank accounts in local currency. Every payment needs the owner's confirmation; an approval threshold adds an extra step; owners can disconnect agents.

## Short quotes

> AgentBank is an MCP server that lets your agent collect money, hold it, convert it, and send it to bank accounts in local currency, within the spending limits and approvals its human owner sets.

> Every payment needs the owner's explicit confirmation of recipient, amounts, fees and expiry before it is created.

> Onboarding returns a browser URL for the owner to approve; agents do not create accounts on their own.

# A more tailored Google Search that’s uniquely yours

**Official source** · Google · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-25 · [open the original](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/google-search-personal-intelligence-apps/)

## Summary

Google blog post dated 23 November 2026, by Robby Stein, VP of Product, Google Search. Users can opt in to connect Google Wallet to Personal Intelligence in Search AI Mode, rolling out from that day, to see spending and get tips on credit-card benefits from cards linked in Wallet. If Wallet is connected, AI Mode can only read information from the accounts; it cannot move, manage, or invest money. Drive and the Gemini app connections to AI Mode are described as following in the coming weeks. The post does not name countries or Plaid.

## Short quotes

> Starting to roll out today, you can opt in to securely connect Google Wallet

> If you choose to connect your Wallet, AI Mode can only read information from your accounts; it can’t move, manage, or invest your money.

> You can also maximize credit card benefits — like optimizing cash-back categories, travel perks, and reward points — based on your cards linked via Wallet.

# Rain Agent Vaults bring embedded payments to AI agents

**Official source** · Rain · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-15 · [open the original](https://www.rain.xyz/resources/rain-agent-vaults-bring-embedded-payments-to-ai-agents)

## Summary

Rain press release (New York, November 10, 2026): Agent Vaults give an AI agent a funded balance, owner-set rules and a Rain-issued scoped Visa or Mastercard card for each purchase, plus bank payouts and stablecoin sends. Funded by bank transfer, card or stablecoins; balances held as stablecoins in a non-custodial wallet. Supports Visa Intelligent Commerce and Mastercard Agent Pay. Sold to companies building agents.

## Short quotes

> A vault is funded by bank transfer, card, or stablecoins, and the agent can only pay or send what is in it.

> As a Visa and Mastercard Principal Member, Rain Agent Vaults enable both Visa Intelligent Commerce and Mastercard Agent Pay, which signal the user’s intent and consent.

> The agent cannot raise its own limit, and a single-use card stops working after one approved purchase.

> Rain is partnering with leading agent developers, marketplaces, and payment platforms on Agent Vaults.

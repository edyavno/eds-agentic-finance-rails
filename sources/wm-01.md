# Walmart’s Black Friday Deals Continue. Meta’s Muse Can Now Shop Them, Too.

**Official source** · Walmart · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-25 · [open the original](https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/11/black-friday-deals-with-meta-muse)

## Summary

Walmart newsroom article dated 24 November 2026. Starting that day, customers can shop Walmart through Meta's Muse via the Walmart Agent Gateway. Muse passes the shopping list to Walmart, which finds matching products. The first time a shopper adds an item, they sign in to Walmart and set up an Agent Profile in the Walmart account, including a spending limit. Shoppers can set spending limits, approve purchases, and check which purchases were made by the agent. Checkout uses a card saved in the Walmart account. Walmart's return policy still applies. The Gateway will support more apps and agents; those other agents are not named.

## Short quotes

> Starting today, customers can shop Walmart’s Black Friday deals directly through [Muse](https://muse.ai/), Meta’s personal AI agent, which can help them find items, build a basket and complete their purchase.

> Instead of searching for each item yourself, Muse connects to the Walmart Agent Gateway, where it passes your shopping list to Walmart. Walmart then does the shopping, finding products that match your request.

> And with Agent Profiles, you’re in control. You can set spending limits, approve purchases and check your purchase history to see which purchases were made by your agent.

> The first time you add an item to your cart, you’ll set up your Agent Profile on Walmart within your Walmart account, including a spending limit you’re comfortable with.

# Put Gemini to work for you

**Official source** · Google · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-27 · [open the original](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/tasks-in-gemini-free-new-features/)

## Summary

Google blog post dated 17 November 2026, by Josh Woodward, VP of Labs, Gemini app, and AI Studio. Tasks in Gemini is rolling out free of charge in the US, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia and India, with a set of advanced features starting in the US. Gemini can check out with a Google Pay integration and, the post says, now uses the Agent Payments Protocol (AP2), with digital tokens on supported Mastercard and Visa cards; American Express is coming soon. The user can set a hard spending cap; receipts go to Google Wallet. Gemini is designed to ask for confirmation or pre-approval before clicking Buy, and a routine action can be set to Always allow. Universal Commerce Protocol support is described as next. Default cap amounts, which merchants, and the countries where checkout with Google Pay is available beyond the US are not stated.

## Short quotes

> Starting now, this update is rolling out free of charge for hundreds of millions of people across the U.S., Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India, using our new Gemini 4 Hydrogen model. We’re also rolling out a set of advanced features, starting in the U.S.

> Gemini now uses the Agent Payments Protocol (AP2) to make purchases like this. We’re working with American Express (coming soon), Mastercard, and Visa to keep your purchases secure with digital tokens on supported cards.

> You can also set a hard spending cap upfront, and Gemini is designed to stay within your budget. All of your receipts and tracking info land straight in Google Wallet

> Gemini is designed to ask for your confirmation or pre-approval before clicking "Buy," and will ask clarifying questions if an instruction is ambiguous. For routine stuff you already trust, like adding those Cheerios to your grocery cart, you can set that to “Always allow” so it doesn’t pester you.

# Stripe helps Meta Muse shop with Link

**Official source** · Stripe · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/stripe-helps-meta-muse-shop-with-link)

## Summary

Stripe press release, 2026-09-08. Meta integrated Link's wallet for agents with Muse for US consumers. At more than 1 million businesses that accept Link, Muse can check out with the payment method saved in Link; elsewhere Link issues a single-use virtual card scoped to the purchase. The consumer approves the transaction total in the chat and Muse never sees the underlying payment details. Stripe says Link's agent wallet also helps power payments for Grok Bot and Instinct.

## Short quotes

# Personal agents are shopping this holiday season

**Official source** · Cloudflare · official-doc · retrieved 2026-11-27 · [open the original](https://radar.cloudflare.com/bfcm/2026)

## Summary

Cloudflare Radar's Black Friday / Cyber Monday 2026 Spotlight page, titled "Personal agents are shopping this holiday season," marked updated hourly. The figures below are what the page showed when it was read at about 1:17 PM ET (America/New_York) on 27 November 2026. Personal-agent share of requests was up from 0.02% in the October 1–7 baseline to 0.05% in the last 7 days. The page showed 98.39% of personal-agent requests allowed and 1.61% blocked. Requests from personal agents to cart, payment and purchase pages in the last 7 days, versus October 1–7, were shown as All personal agents +535%, Muse +919%, dots +148%, Gemini Tasks +784%, and Other +209%. The United States was shown as 75.4% of global all-personal-agent traffic. "Other" groups Cloudsurf, DuckAssistbot, and ZipchatBot.

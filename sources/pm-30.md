# Give your agents controlled access to analyze your data and move funds

**Official source** · Stripe · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-23 · [open the original](https://stripe.com/blog/give-your-agents-controlled-access-to-analyze-your-data-and-move-funds)

## Summary

Stripe blog post dated 19 November 2026. It is about Stripe businesses, not consumer accounts. Stripe says more than 24,000 businesses already use Stripe Treasury for agents with read access to financial data. The post says Stripe is adding write access and customizable controls so agents can transfer funds, convert currencies, and pay vendors within limits the business sets. Businesses connect a compatible AI tool, such as Claude or Codex, through Stripe's MCP server and choose what the agent can see, which actions it can take, and when human approval is required. If the agent is connected through OAuth as an authorized user, the business must confirm every action that moves money. An agent can also have its own identity through an agent API key; by default, agent-initiated refunds, payouts, and outbound transfers require human approval, and businesses can adapt those rules, for example automatically processing an ACH transfer under $50. Stripe says these agentic money movement capabilities are in public preview.

# Plaid and Perplexity expand integration to power personalized financial insights

**Official source** · Plaid · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://plaid.com/blog/plaid-perplexity-ai-financial-insights-integration/)

## Summary

Plaid blog post, 2026-04-09, about its integration with Perplexity (Plaid's account of the product; Perplexity's own page could not be fetched). Plaid says Perplexity users can connect more account types through Plaid beyond investments, and that Perplexity Computer Pro and Max users can link credit cards, connect mortgages, auto loans and student loans, and combine accounts for a net worth view and build tools such as a budget tracker or debt payoff planner.

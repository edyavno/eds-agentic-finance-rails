## Summary

PYMNTS summary of the Wall Street Journal report of 2026-11-13. It says JPMorgan Chase is blocking AI agents from shopping with its customers' credit cards when customers give the agent their card number, and that cards from Bank of America, Capital One and Citigroup are already being used by agents when customers allow it. It says Stripe's Link wallet gives the agent a one-time virtual card for one purchase and one amount, then bills the shopper's own card, and that Link also supports Visa and Mastercard agentic tokens, which JPMorgan has been slow to adopt.

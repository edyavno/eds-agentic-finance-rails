# Our approach to Agentic AI

**Official source** · Financial Conduct Authority · official-doc · retrieved 2026-11-25 · [open the original](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/innovation/ai-approach/agentic-ai)

## Summary

UK Financial Conduct Authority page, first published and last updated 24 November 2026. The FCA tells technology providers considering a UK agentic product to consider whether they are carrying out regulated activities, including payments services, financial promotions, credit broking or intermediation, or financial advice. It says it has powers to intervene if regulated financial services are offered without authorisation and will not hesitate to use them if necessary. Regulated firms should consider how they know they are dealing with an agent, what consents are in place, what the agent may do, and whether it is appropriate to accept payments instructed by an AI agent, or from a digital wallet controlled by an AI agent. A linked DRCF joint statement PDF is not used here.

## Short quotes

> We have powers to intervene if we believe regulated financial services are being offered without our authorisation and will not hesitate to use them if necessary.

> You should consider your systems and controls, any liabilities that may arise from accepting instructions and consider whether it is appropriate to accept payments instructed by an AI agent, or from a digital wallet controlled by an AI agent.

> This could, for example, include consideration about whether you are offering payments services, issuing financial promotions, providing an authorised information service, engaging in credit broking or intermediation or facilitating or offering financial advice.

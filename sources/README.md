# Sources

Every fact in this wiki is tied to an outside page. A **source card** is a short note about one such page: its link, its publisher, whether it is official or third-party, the date it was retrieved, a summary and a few short quotes. A card is never a full copy of the page.

## How do I check a claim?

A footnote such as `[^rh-01]` marks the card behind a fact. The footnote id is the card id, and the Sources list at the bottom of each page links to the card. Open the card to see the original link and the date we retrieved it. If a field says "unknown", no source confirms it yet.

Cards marked as prior art are leads, not evidence. They point to places worth checking and are never cited for a fact. The [glossary](../glossary.md) defines terms used on the cards, such as [MCP](../glossary.md#mcp).

## Which sources are there?

The cards are grouped by topic. Within a group they are sorted by subject, such as a provider's name.

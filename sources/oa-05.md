# Finances in ChatGPT (OpenAI Help Center)

**Official source** · OpenAI · support · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001222-finances-in-chatgpt)

## Summary

OpenAI's help article for Finances in ChatGPT. It is available in the US to Free, Go, Plus and Pro users on web, iOS and Android. People connect financial accounts through Plaid and credit report data through Experian, as separate connections. Through Finances, ChatGPT cannot move money, pay bills, change account settings, make trades, change retirement contributions, open or close accounts or file taxes; the article says these limits do not describe a separate third-party app. People can disconnect Finances in Settings and delete financial memories; temporary chats do not access connected accounts.

## Short quotes

> Finances is available in the U.S. to Free, Go, Plus, and Pro users on web, iOS, and Android.

> You can connect financial accounts through Plaid and credit report data through Experian.

> Disconnecting Finances removes your connected financial data from ChatGPT.

> Temporary chats: When you use temporary chats, ChatGPT won’t access your connected financial accounts

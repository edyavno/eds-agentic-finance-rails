# Choosing how to initialize products (Plaid Docs)

**Official source** · Plaid Docs · official-doc · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://plaid.com/docs/link/initializing-products/)

## Summary

Plaid's guide to the products, optional products and additional consented products arrays used when an app creates a link_token. Additional consented products collect the person's consent now for a product the app may use later; Plaid does not extract data for them until the app calls their endpoints.

## Short quotes

> Plaid will not extract data for these products and Item creation will not fail if the products are unavailable or incompatible.

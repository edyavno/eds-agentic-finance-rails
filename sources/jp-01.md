# JPMorganChase and Plaid announce an extension to their data access agreement

**Official source** · JPMorganChase · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/newsroom/press-releases/2025/jpmc-plaid-renewed-data-access-agreement)

## Summary

JPMorganChase press release, 2025-09-16, with Plaid. The two say they renewed a data access agreement that continues to let shared customers access products they rely on. The release says the agreement includes a pricing structure and sets out commitments on safe, secure and consistent access; it gives no price. It says the agreement would not affect Plaid's current customer agreements and pricing.

## Short quotes

> The agreement, which includes a pricing structure, outlines a series of commitments that JPMC and Plaid made to ensure that consumers can access their data safely, securely, quickly and consistently into the future.

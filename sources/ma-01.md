# Keynote address by the Managing Director of the Monetary Authority of Singapore at the Singapore FinTech Festival 2026

**Official source** · Monetary Authority of Singapore · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-20 · [open the original](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/speeches/2026/keynote-address-by-md-at-the-singapore-fintech-festival-2026)

## Summary

Speech by the Managing Director of the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) at the Singapore FinTech Festival on 2026-11-19. In the AI part, MAS says some users give agents their login credentials to financial accounts and that it would not advise this. It says banks cannot tell whether an agent has the customer's authorisation or is acting within its mandate. It says that in the new phase of its Safeguards for Agentic Finance at Runtime (SAFR) work it will facilitate a Know Your Agent protocol, and that it will set up an agentic money sandbox with the Future of Finance Institute for two use cases, corporate treasury management and payments.

## Short quotes

> Some users are giving agents their login credentials to financial accounts. I would not advise giving personal agents such access, as there is insufficient assurance of safety.

> In the new phase of SAFR, we will facilitate the development of know your agent protocol for trusted information exchange between customer agents and financial institutions.

> We will also set up a Sandbox with the Future of Finance Institute. The sandbox will bring two technologies together – AI Agents and Programmable Money, and apply them to two use cases – corporate treasury management and payments services.

> We will need clear identity, authority and mandate for each agent, and in each financial institution, we need terms, channels and controls to accept agent instructions.

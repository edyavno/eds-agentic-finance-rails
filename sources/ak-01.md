# Make Holiday Shopping Agent-Ready with Visa TAP

**Official source** · Akamai · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-25 · [open the original](https://www.akamai.com/blog/security/make-holiday-shopping-agent-ready-visa-tap)

## Summary

Akamai blog post dated 24 November 2026, by Emily Lyons. In Akamai's own account, agents registered with Visa's Trusted Agent Protocol are now identified in Akamai's bot directory. Akamai says it verifies TAP signatures at the edge against credentials tied to Visa's agent directory, and that merchants can let a verified agent browse while applying stricter rules to checkout, login, or account changes.

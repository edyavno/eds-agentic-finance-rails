# Vault and secure sign-in (Grok Bot docs)

**Official source** · xAI Docs · official-doc · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/vault)

## Summary

Grok Bot's Vault page. Vault stores logins, cards and contact info so a Bot can sign in and check out without seeing passwords. Settings > Vault also lists payment accounts (wallets you can connect, such as Link, X Money or Shop Pay) and a default payment method that Grok Bot pays with first. Vault covers the whole account, so everything saved is available to every Bot on it.

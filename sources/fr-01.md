# Speech by Governor Barr: Some Potential Financial Stability Implications of Artificial Intelligence

**Official source** · Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System · official-doc · retrieved 2026-11-23 · [open the original](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/barr20261116a.htm)

## Summary

Speech by Federal Reserve Governor Michael S. Barr on 16 November 2026, at the Seventh Annual Association for Computing Machinery International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Finance, in Milan, Italy. Most of the speech is about financial stability and is out of scope for this wiki. In a short passage on new consumer-account risks, Barr says major banks now report finding AI agents attempting to take actions in retail customer accounts, without the bank being able to verify authenticity, consent, and alignment with customer instructions. He says those incidents underscore the need for strict authentication, explicit consent verification for specific actions, and technical controls limiting agent capabilities, among other guardrails.

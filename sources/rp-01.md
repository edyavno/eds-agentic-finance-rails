# Replit Agent can now buy for your business and help you reach customers

**Official source** · Replit · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-20 · [open the original](https://replit.com/blog/replit-agent-ads-commerce)

## Summary

Replit blog post of 2026-11-12. Replit Agent can now buy supplies for a business using Link, built by Stripe, and Visa. The Agent prepares the merchant's checkout and waits for the owner to approve the final total before anything is charged. The browser session is isolated and the Agent never gets open-ended permission to spend. It is available for Core and Pro users, starting in the US and limited to one-time purchases.

## Short quotes

> Then it waits for you to approve the final total before anything is charged.

> Link facilitates the transaction scoped to the approved purchase, while Visa provides trusted payment infrastructure and capabilities designed to support secure agent-initiated transactions.

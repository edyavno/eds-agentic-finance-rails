# How Muse works with payments (Meta Help Center)

**Official source** · Meta · support · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://www.meta.com/help/superintelligence/1436362127544482/)

## Summary

Meta's help page on Muse payments. Muse can help make purchases online and always asks for approval before completing one. It can pay with Link by Stripe (a one-time card number per purchase) or by logging in with credentials saved in the Secure Credential Store and paying with a saved credit card. The person is responsible for transactions Muse makes. It does not mention paying from a bank account. The fingerprint covers the retained excerpt (reader fetch).

# Booking.com: Terms and Conditions (Customer terms of service)

**Official source** · Booking.com · terms · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://www.booking.com/content/terms.html)

## Summary

Booking.com's Customer terms of service, which the page marks "Updated August 25, 2025". Section A15 (Intellectual property rights) bars accessing, scraping or otherwise using the Platform through robots or other automated means, including automated assistants such as AI-powered assistants that work through a user's browser, without Booking.com's prior, express written permission. It adds that Booking.com does not authorize automated assistants to make bookings or reservations. The same section says Booking.com will block anyone it suspects of using automated assistants, including AI-powered assistants, without express permission.

## Short quotes

> Updated August 25, 2025

> you’re not allowed to access, monitor, copy, scrape/crawl, download, reproduce, or otherwise use anything on our Platform using any robot, spider, scraper, other automated means, or automated assistants

> Booking.com does not authorize the use of automated means or automated assistants (including, but not limited to, those that operate by interacting with or otherwise making use of your browser, such as AI-powered assistants) to make bookings or reservations

> using automated assistants (including AI-powered assistants) to access the Platform without express permission

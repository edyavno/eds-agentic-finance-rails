## Short quotes

> Wallet is available for US Pro users with eligible US-issued American Express, Chase, Mastercard and Visa cards. To add an eligible Chase card, you must be the primary cardholder, not an authorized user.

> Approving a purchase requires biometric verification, such as Face ID or Touch ID, on a supported device.

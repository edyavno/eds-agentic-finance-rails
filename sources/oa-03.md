# ChatGPT Wallet terms

**Official source** · OpenAI · terms · retrieved 2026-11-15 · [open the original](https://openai.com/policies/chatgpt-wallet-terms/)

## Summary

ChatGPT Wallet terms (effective November 12, 2026). Wallet holds no balance and extends no credit; the user approves each purchase or grants ChatGPT authority to approve purchases in advance. The issuer decides whether to authorize, and the merchant whether to accept.

## Short quotes

> ChatGPT Wallet does not create a bank account or stored balance, extend credit, transfer funds, or change your relationship with your card issuer.

> You must review and approve each purchase before it is submitted or grant ChatGPT the authority to approve each purchase in advance.

> Your issuer decides whether to authorize the transaction, and the merchant decides whether to accept and fulfill the order.

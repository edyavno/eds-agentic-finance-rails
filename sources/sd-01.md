# Know Your Human (KYH): Fraud prevention for agentic commerce

**Official source** · Sardine · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-20 · [open the original](https://www.sardine.ai/blog/know-your-human-agentic-commerce)

## Summary

Sardine blog post of 2026-11-09 describing its Know Your Human (KYH) product. Sardine says shopping agents convert poorly because merchants mistake them for bots, and that agent purchases leave merchants without the device signals needed to defend a chargeback. It says KYH binds the customer's own device to the agent's session through a short-lived link and a one-time code. These are Sardine's own claims about its own product.

## Short quotes

> Sardine’s Know Your Human (KYH) binds the customer’s own device to the agent’s session.

> Shopping agents often convert poorly because merchants mistake them for malicious bots and decline the order.

> The merchant sends a secure, short-lived link to the customer by SMS or email.

> KYH records the customer’s device ID, fingerprint, and IP address against the agent’s session. These provide the device or IP match required for CE 3.0 when they match qualifying prior transactions, and can satisfy the device factor under Mastercard First-Party Trust.

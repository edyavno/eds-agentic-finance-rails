# How do I connect to Scalable MCP? | Scalable Capital Help Center

**Official source** · Scalable Capital Help Center · support · retrieved 2026-11-19 · [open the original](https://help.scalable.capital/en/agentic-investing-aac939f8/how-do-i-connect-to-scalable-mcp-75fc6a40)

## Summary

Scalable MCP setup: server https://mcp.scalable.capital/mcp; access level chosen in the web app under Profile > Security > Agentic Investing (web version only) from "No access", "Read-only", "Limited changes" or "Full access"; the level applies globally to all assistants and portfolios; browser sign-in with two-factor authentication; per-app steps for ChatGPT, ChatGPT Desktop, Claude, Google Gemini, Grok, Cursor and Google Antigravity.

## Short quotes

> You can select “No access”, “Read-only”, “Limited changes” or “Full access”.

> Your selected MCP access level applies globally to your Scalable MCP access. It therefore applies to all AI assistants connected to Scalable MCP and to all portfolios available through Scalable MCP. Different access levels cannot be set for individual AI assistants or portfolios.

> Never share your Scalable password or backup code in an AI chat.

> Also, ensure that you do not approve any two-factor authentication requests that you did not initiate yourself.

# Scheme: exact on Bitcoin Lightning (lnbtc)

**Official source** · x402 Foundation · official-doc · retrieved 2026-11-27 · [open the original](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/x402-foundation/x402/main/specs/schemes/exact/scheme_exact_lnbtc.md)

## Summary

x402 Foundation specification for the exact payment scheme on Bitcoin Lightning, under the lnbtc namespace, targeting x402 protocol version 2. The client pays a fresh BOLT11 invoice from the resource server and returns the 32-byte payment preimage. The facilitator checks that the SHA-256 of the preimage matches the payment hash, and that the invoice signing key matches payTo. The scheme describes this machine-to-machine payment flow. It does not describe a person approving a payment.

## Short quotes

> The client pays a fresh BOLT11 invoice from the resource server and returns its 32-byte payment preimage.

> The facilitator verifies `SHA-256(preimage) == payment_hash` and requires the invoice signing key to match `payTo`.

> A client MUST NOT select this scheme unless its payer Lightning node returns the preimage after payment.

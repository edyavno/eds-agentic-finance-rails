# Perplexity brings Personal CFO to Europe with Plaid

**Official source** · Plaid · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://plaid.com/en-eu/blog/perplexity-personal-cfo-europe/)

## Summary

Plaid blog post, 2026-11-12 (Plaid's account of the product; Perplexity's own page could not be fetched). Plaid says people in 17 European countries can ask Perplexity questions based on their own bank account data through Personal CFO, with Plaid powering the connection. Plaid says people choose which accounts to share, access is read-only, and in the EU Plaid provides the connection as a regulated account information service through Plaid B.V.

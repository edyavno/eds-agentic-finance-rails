# More banks say customers using AI agents are on their own — even if they get hacked (TechCrunch)

**Third-party source** · TechCrunch · news · retrieved 2026-11-20 · [open the original](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/more-banks-says-customers-using-ai-agents-are-on-their-own-even-if-they-get-hacked/)

## Summary

TechCrunch report of 2026-11-18. It says Citi changed its terms that week so that customers who use third-party AI tools for personal finance are responsible for what goes wrong, even if they are hacked. It says JPMorgan and Bank of America announced similar policies "last month" and that PNC changed its terms on the day of the article. It says Citizens emailed its customers with a similar warning, according to an email TechCrunch saw.

## Short quotes

> Citibank, the third largest commercial bank in the United States, changed its terms of service this week, telling customers who use third-party AI tools for personal finance that anything that goes wrong is their problem — even if they get hacked.

> JPMorgan and Bank of America, the two largest banks in the country, announced similar policies last month. And PNC, the eighth largest bank, changed its terms today.

> Banking giant Citizens similarly emailed its customers this week, warning that they are also on the hook for anything their AI assistant does, according to an email seen by TechCrunch.

> Citi’s terms note that AI agents it provides can be used by customers without worrying about additional liability.

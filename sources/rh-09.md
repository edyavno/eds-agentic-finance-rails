# Agentic Credit Card | Robinhood

**Official source** · Robinhood · support · retrieved 2026-11-19 · [open the original](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/agentic-credit-card/)

## Summary

Agentic Credit Card: virtual card under Robinhood Gold Card via Robinhood Banking MCP; choose approval for every purchase or a required monthly spending limit. The 2026-11-19 re-read adds the Banking MCP URL (https://banking-agent.robinhood.com/mcp/banking), per-platform connection steps (Claude Code, Claude Desktop, ChatGPT, Codex, Codex CLI, Cursor, OpenClaw), a desktop-only setup note, and the rule that monthly limits are required if you do not approve each purchase.

## Short quotes

> Monthly limits are required if you choose not to approve each purchase.

> You can only create an agentic credit card and authenticate your agent on a desktop device. If you’re connecting to the Robinhood Banking MCP on a mobile device, copy the onboarding URL and open it in a desktop browser.

> Your agent can only access the details of the agentic virtual cards you authorize. It can’t access or view anything else across your Robinhood account.

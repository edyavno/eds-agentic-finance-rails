# What can my AI assistant do with the Scalable MCP? | Scalable Capital Help Center

**Official source** · Scalable Capital Help Center · support · retrieved 2026-11-19 · [open the original](https://help.scalable.capital/en/agentic-investing-aac939f8/what-can-my-ai-assistant-do-with-the-scalable-mcp-28d8129c)

## Summary

What the assistant can do per access level; supported buy and sell orders use a mandatory two-step process (assistant prepares an order preview, you give a fresh, explicit confirmation in a separate step). Tool tables cover account/portfolio, portfolio groups, market data, watchlists, price alerts, savings plans, orders and a ping tool. Personal Broker, Overnight and Fixed-Term accounts supported; Junior accounts not.

## Short quotes

> You can retrieve supported information about your account, portfolios and markets. Changes to watchlists, price alerts, portfolio groups and savings plans, as well as buy and sell orders, are not available.

> In addition to retrieving information, you can manage your watchlists, price alerts and portfolio groups. Changes to savings plans and buy or sell orders are not available.

> All functions supported by Scalable MCP are available. This includes changes to savings plans and supported buy and sell orders.

> Scalable MCP supports personal Broker, Overnight and Fixed-Term accounts. Junior accounts are not supported.

**Official source** · OpenAI · support · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001530-getting-started-with-your-dot)

## Summary

OpenAI's setup guide for dots. Dots are rolling out to Pro users aged 18 and over outside the EEA, Switzerland and the UK, to Business Premium users in supported regions, and to Enterprise, Edu and Healthcare workspaces (default on from 2026-11-18 unless an admin opts out). Users choose what the dot can do on its own, can pause it at any time, and can set rules for what it can share, purchase or access.

# Lianlian DigiTech Launches LianLian XCard

**Official source** · Lianlian DigiTech (PR Newswire) · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-27 · [open the original](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/lianlian-digitech-launches-lianlian-xcard-closing-the-loop-between-ai-task-execution-and-agentic-payments-302950355.html)

## Summary

Lianlian DigiTech press release dated 25 November 2026, issued through PR Newswire. It announces LianLian XCard, a business card that, the release says, lets authorised AI agents initiate payments within predefined authorisation limits. Since 18 November, eligible users can apply through LianLian XWorker, link the card to the LianLian Agent Wallet, and set purchase intent, permissions and budgets. Payments through LianLian XPay can use single-use payment tokens and dynamic security credentials, without the agents accessing the card numbers. The release says the launch of LianLian XCard is powered by Visa Intelligent Commerce Connect. That Visa claim is Lianlian DigiTech's, in this release. Fees and which countries can use the card are not stated here.

## Short quotes

> The launch of LianLian XCard, powered by Visa Intelligent Commerce Connect, further extends the collaboration into business spending and expense management.

> Since November 18, eligible users have been able to apply for LianLian XCard through Lianlian DigiTech's AI Workforce platform, LianLian XWorker.

> When payments are executed through LianLian XPay, transactions can be completed using single-use payment tokens and dynamic security credentials, without requiring AI agents to access actual card numbers.

> LianLian XCard enables authorised AI agents to initiate payments within predefined authorisation limits

# Agent Accounts (Lightspark documentation)

**Official source** · Lightspark (documentation) · official-doc · retrieved 2026-11-20 · [open the original](https://docs.lightspark.com/agent-accounts)

## Summary

Lightspark documentation overview of Agent Accounts. A customer connects an AI agent to their account for work such as getting paid, paying people and suppliers, and buying online. Lightspark's Grid checks every agent action against the customer's policy and runs it, refuses it or holds it for approval. AI companies can list Lightspark as a connector that their assistant calls over MCP, and Grid platforms use an Agents API that must be enabled per platform.

## Short quotes

> The Agents API must be enabled for your platform; until it is, agent endpoints answer `403`.

> Grid checks every agent action against the policy the customer set, and either runs it, refuses it, or holds it for the customer to approve.

> List Lightspark as a connector in your app. Your users sign up, verify and approve payments with Lightspark, and your assistant calls Lightspark over MCP.

> The customer can pause the agent, revoke the key, or delete the agent at any time.

# How Muse works with Connectors (Meta Help Center)

**Official source** · Meta · support · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://www.meta.com/help/superintelligence/1687253048996149/)

## Summary

Meta's help page on Muse Connectors. Connectors give Muse new abilities; by default Muse will not take many important actions without approval, many Connectors can be limited to retrieving data, and people can disconnect a Connector at any time in Settings (data already used may remain in Muse's memories and conversation history). Muse can create custom connectors that Meta does not review. It does not name a financial or Plaid connector. The fingerprint covers the retained excerpt (reader fetch).

## Short quotes

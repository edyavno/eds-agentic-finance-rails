# PayPal Agentic Commerce Merchant Virtual Card Terms

**Official source** · PayPal · terms · retrieved 2026-11-25 · [open the original](https://www.paypalobjects.com/marketing/ua/OA/agentic-commerce-merchant-vc-tnc/en-US-112326.pdf)

## Summary

PayPal terms last updated 23 November 2026, for merchants with a PayPal business account when a buyer pays with PayPal through an artificial intelligence agent commerce service and PayPal settles to the merchant with a one-time virtual card (an Agentic VC). The merchant is responsible for card-processor fees on the Agentic VC, must refund to the same card, and must show the buyer's payment method as "PayPal". These payments are not eligible under PayPal's Seller Protection program. Disputes go through the card network's chargeback process. Which agent services trigger an Agentic VC is determined by PayPal and is not listed here.

## Short quotes

> PayPal payments received from a buyer using an Agentic Commerce Service that are settled by PayPal to you through an Agentic VC are not eligible transactions under PayPal’s Seller Protection program.

> Disputes associated with a PayPal payment received from a buyer using an Agentic Commerce Service that is settled by PayPal to you through an Agentic VC are resolved through the applicable Agentic VC’s payment card network’s chargeback process.

> must identify the payment method as “PayPal” anywhere you show the buyer’s payment method, including on order pages, order confirmations, and receipts.

> You must process any refund, partial refund, or price adjustment for a PayPal payment received from a buyer using an Agentic Commerce Service that is settled by PayPal to you through an Agentic VC as a credit to the same Agentic VC, through the card processor that processed the original transaction.

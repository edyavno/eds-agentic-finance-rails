# Coinbase for Agents introduces new Agent Controls

**Official source** · Coinbase · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://www.coinbase.com/blog/agent-controls)

## Summary

Coinbase blog post, dated 2026-11-11, announcing Agent Controls for Coinbase for Agents. Controls run on Coinbase's servers and are managed in the Coinbase app or on the web, so the agent cannot bypass or change them. They are a maximum trade size in USD, a 24-hour volume limit (a trade over the limit won't go through) and an auto-stop that makes the agent stop placing trades when a portfolio is down more than a set percentage, until the user turns trading back on in the Coinbase app. Product permissions (crypto, equities, derivatives and more) are marked coming soon. The post does not say whether auto-stop cancels open orders. The page blocks plain scripted requests (HTTP 403); the text was read through a reader fetch.

## Short quotes

> These controls run on Coinbase's servers, not inside your agent, so your agent can't bypass them.

> Maximum trade size: Set a limit in USD for each trade.

> Auto-stop: Set a percentage-loss threshold for a portfolio. If your portfolio falls below it, your agent stops placing trades until you turn trading back on in the Coinbase app.

> Product permissions [coming soon] : Choose which product categories your agent can trade between: crypto, equities, derivatives and more.

# Citi Online User Agreement (Section H: Use of Third-party AI Agents)

**Official source** · Citi · terms · retrieved 2026-11-20 · [open the original](https://www.citi.com/tandc/view-printable-version)

## Summary

Citi's Online User Agreement, which says it was last updated November 2026 to add Section H on third-party AI agents. Section H cautions customers against using third-party AI agents with Citi Online and Citi Mobile. It says that if a customer gives their Online Credentials to such an agent, Citi will consider the agent's access and actions authorized by the customer, including after the credentials are further shared or leaked. It says the terms do not apply to AI agents or tools Citi provides itself.

## Short quotes

> This agreement was last updated November 2026 to add a new Section H titled Use of Third-Party Artificial Intelligence (“AI”) Agents.

> Citi will consider any access and actions taken by the agent as authorized by You.

> If the Online Credentials that you provided to the third-party AI Agent are further shared, leaked, or otherwise distributed to other persons or AI agents, Citi will consider any access and actions taken with the Online Credentials as authorized by You.

> For the avoidance of doubt, these terms do not apply to your use of any third-party AI Agent, tool, function or feature provided by Citi through Citi Online or Citi Mobile.

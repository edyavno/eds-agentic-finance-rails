# Get info & personalized insights from Google Wallet with Gemini Apps

**Official source** · Google Gemini Apps Help · support · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/18112192?hl=en)

## Summary

Google's Gemini Apps Help page for the Google Wallet connected app. Gemini can give insights from transaction data of financial accounts linked through services like Plaid, summarize expenses and give budgeting tips, and summarize rewards and offers for cards and passes in Wallet. It requires a user aged 18 or over in the US with a personal Google Account and History on. It is English only, in the Gemini mobile app and web app, and not in Gems or Gemini Live. Google says it is being made available gradually. Gemini cannot make transactions with payment methods in Wallet, and cannot update or delete connected financial accounts (that is done in Google Wallet settings).

## Short quotes

> Make transactions using payment methods in your Google Wallet.

> Update or delete connected financial accounts.

> We’re gradually making this feature available, so it might not be available to you just yet.

> Be 18 or over and in the US.

# Link OAuth guide (Plaid Docs)

**Official source** · Plaid Docs · official-doc · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://plaid.com/docs/link/oauth/)

## Summary

Plaid's OAuth guide. With OAuth the person leaves Link, signs in and approves data sharing on the bank's own website or app, then returns to Link. Plaid says OAuth lets people grant access without sharing credentials with the third party, and that OAuth support is required for integrations that connect to US, EU and UK institutions.

## Short quotes

> With OAuth, end users can grant third parties access to their data without sharing their credentials directly with the third party.

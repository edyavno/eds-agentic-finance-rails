# Cashfree Wallet

**Official source** · Cashfree · official-doc · retrieved 2026-11-25 · [open the original](https://www.cashfree.com/cashfree-wallet/)

## Summary

Cashfree's consumer page for Cashfree Wallet. The agent pays from the wallet and never sees the user's card, UPI or bank details. The user links the wallet to an agent with a mobile OTP, sets a budget (amount, number of payments, and how long consent lasts), can revoke access anytime, and gets an alert for every payment. Bills (electricity, Wi-Fi, mobile) are live; travel, food, groceries and shopping are coming soon. The wallet is in invite-only beta. Cashfree says 100+ agents are already integrated. The page has no publication or launch date.

## Short quotes

> Your agent never sees your card, UPI or bank details.

> Pick the budget, number of payments and how long consent lasts. Revoke your agent's access anytime.

> Link Cashfree Wallet to your agent with a mobile OTP.

> Cashfree Wallet is in invite-only beta.

## Summary

A post of 2026-11-20 by Ernie Tedeschi, Stripe's chief economist, on his personal X account (his bio says "Personal account. Chief economist at @Stripe"). It is not an official Stripe release. The post links to his X Article, "[The agentic story on the eve of the 2026 holiday season](https://x.com/i/article/2123849094036881408)". The article says daily spend on Link Agent Wallet, which powers payments for personal agents including Muse, Instinct, and Grok, was nearly 80-fold higher in mid-November than at the start of September, measured using a seven-day rolling average, and that spend doubled in the last month. It says last week over 40% of Link agentic spending volume was on airfare, travel, and lodging, roughly a third on retail, and a quarter on other categories, and that more than half of consumers who make one agentic purchase come back to make at least a second, typically in the same spending category.

# Contract of Carriage Document | United Airlines

**Official source** · United Airlines · terms · retrieved 2026-11-15 · [open the original](https://www.united.com/en/us/fly/contract-of-carriage.html)

## Summary

United's Contract of Carriage, revised November 11, 2026. Defines "AI Agent" broadly (whether or not a person supervises or approves its actions) and lists the use of AI Agents to make, modify or service a ticket, or to buy any service or product, among prohibited practices in Rule 6.

## Short quotes

> (revised November 11, 2026)

> The use of AI Agents to make, modify or service a Ticket, and/or to purchase any service or product.

> AI Agent means any software or service, including but not limited to an AI assistant, AI agent or AI-enabled browser that takes autonomous or semi-autonomous action, whether or not Passenger or another individual supervises or approves its actions

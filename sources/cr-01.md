# A Consumer-First Standard for AI Payments (Affirm and Consumer Reports case study)

**Official source** · Affirm and Consumer Reports · official-doc · retrieved 2026-11-23 · [open the original](https://innovation.consumerreports.org/Affirm-CR-CaseStudy-Nov2026.pdf)

## Summary

A case study PDF from Affirm and Consumer Reports, titled "A Consumer-First Standard for AI Payments." It describes a draft set of written instructions that helps an AI assistant recommend how to pay for a purchase, tested against Consumer Reports' Consumer Finance AI Standard. The framework guides five steps: verify and contextualize, normalize, stress-test, gate and rank, and recommend. It is not a financial adviser or a finished consumer product and cannot access accounts or make purchases on its own. Testing used 30 payment-advice tasks, with and without the framework, across four model configurations (GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-5.6 Luna, Claude Sonnet 5.5, Claude Haiku 5.5). Each of the four models attempted the 30 tasks three times per condition, producing 720 conversations. Pass rates were higher with the framework; the equal-model average was 48.6% without it and 71.8% with it, an increase of 23.2 percentage points. The PDF says the results do not establish improved consumer outcomes or readiness for consumer use, and that further testing is needed before use in real-world consumer experiences. The PDF itself does not carry a publication date.

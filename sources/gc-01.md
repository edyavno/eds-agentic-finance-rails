# Take payments through AI agents

**Official source** · GoCardless · official-doc · retrieved 2026-11-27 · [open the original](https://gocardless.com/go/agentic-payments)

## Summary

GoCardless product page for agentic payments. A customer's AI agent calls a GoCardless MCP, a secure form opens, and the agent does not get access to the bank details. Customers remain in control of payments, with final approval. The rails named are Direct Debit and open banking. GoCardless says it was selected for the Financial Conduct Authority's Live AI Testing Programme, and that agentic commerce is in early access for UK merchants taking payments in GBP; merchants should speak to the team to get it enabled. The page distinguishes a merchant-facing GoCardless MCP from a commerce MCP for payers. The MCP address and fees are not stated.

## Short quotes

> Customers remain in control of payments, with final approval

> We are one of a handful of businesses selected to take part in the Financial Conduct Authority’s Live AI Testing Programme.

> It is in early access for UK merchants taking payments in GBP. Speak to our team to get it enabled on your account.

> They are two different MCPs. The GoCardless MCP is for merchants, so you can manage your own GoCardless account with an AI assistant. The commerce MCP powers agentic commerce and is for your payers to interact with, so they can choose and pay for your products inside your chat.

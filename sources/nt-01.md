# NETS and Ant International Deepen Partnership to Empower Singapore’s Businesses for a Future in Agentic Commerce with Agentic Mobile Protocol

**Official source** · NETS (Network for Electronic Transfers) · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-20 · [open the original](https://www.nets.com.sg/newsroom/nets-and-ant-international-deepen-partnership-to-empower-singapores-businesses-for-a-future-in-agentic-commerce-with-agentic-mobile-protocol)

## Summary

NETS media release of 2026-11-19. NETS and Ant International signed a Memorandum of Understanding at the Singapore Fintech Festival 2026 to explore a collaboration around NETS' digital payments services and Ant International's Agentic Mobile Protocol (AMP). NETS will become the first national payment rail to join AMP. The two will explore NETS App wallet users authorizing AI agents to pay, NETS deploying AMP in its merchant acquiring, and interoperability between Know-Your-Agent frameworks.

## Short quotes

> NETS and Ant International signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) at the Singapore Fintech Festival 2026 to explore a collaboration that draws on NETS’ digital payments services and Ant International’s Agentic Mobile Protocol (AMP) as well as its full stack of AI-native solutions.

> NETS will become the first national payment rail to join Ant International’s AMP, an agentic payment protocol designed for mobile interfaces.

> Additionally, Ant International and NETS will explore interoperability between Know-Your-Agent (KYA) frameworks to enable more efficient onboarding and strengthen trust, accountability and risk visibility across both ecosystems.

> Launched in April 2026, AMP is an open payment protocol built by Ant International for digital wallets, super apps, smart devices and other mobile interfaces to enable AI agents to execute payments.

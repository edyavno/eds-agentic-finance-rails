# Terms & Conditions : Target

**Official source** · Target · terms · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://www.target.com/c/terms-conditions/-/N-4sr7l)

## Summary

Target's website Terms & Conditions, which the page marks "LAST UPDATED: September 10, 2026". Its "Agentic Commerce and Delegated Access" section lets an AI agent act in a Target account only if the customer expressly authorizes it and Target has approved it (an "Approved Agent"). The current list is Target's AI Shopping Assistant, OAI, Google and Microsoft, and Target can change it at any time. All other agentic commerce tools are prohibited from accessing, logging in to, scraping or querying the site. Approved Agents may sign in, change carts, place orders and start returns within limits the customer approves; the customer stays responsible for reviewing activity, and Target can suspend or revoke an agent's access.

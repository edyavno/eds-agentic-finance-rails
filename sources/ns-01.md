# Frequently asked questions | Nous

**Official source** · Nous · official-doc · retrieved 2026-11-15 · [open the original](https://www.nous.co/faqs)

## Summary

Nous FAQ: a UK household AI agent for bills and life admin. Three savings modes (Manual, Balanced, Freedom) set how much it does on its own. Free and Premium (£6.99 per household a month) tiers for one UK address. Open banking through an FCA authorised provider; Nous says money cannot be moved or transferred.

## Short quotes

> Nous is a household AI agent that saves you money, time and hassle by taking care of bills and life admin

> Nous offers open banking enabled services through an FCA authorised and regulated open banking provider.

# Plaid is powering personalized financial insights with Google Wallet in the Gemini app

**Official source** · Plaid · announcement · retrieved 2026-11-18 · [open the original](https://plaid.com/blog/google-wallet-gemini-plaid/)

## Summary

Plaid blog post, 2026-11-13. Plaid says it powers account linking in Google Wallet for the Gemini app: people can choose to link bank accounts, credit cards, loans and investment accounts to Google Wallet through Plaid. Plaid repeats that Gemini has read-only access and cannot move, manage or invest money, and that people can manage their Plaid connections in Plaid Portal. It is rolling out to Gemini app users in the U.S.

## Short quotes

> People can choose to link their bank accounts, credit cards, loans and investment accounts to Google Wallet through Plaid for deeper insights.

> Gemini has read-only access: it cannot move, manage, or invest their money.

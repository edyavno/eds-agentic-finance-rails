# Agent Ready overview | PayPal Developer

**Official source** · PayPal Developer · official-doc · retrieved 2026-11-23 · [open the original](https://developer.paypal.com/agent-ready/overview)

## Summary

PayPal Developer documentation for Agent Ready, last updated 13 October 2026. Agent Ready helps Braintree and PayPal merchants accept payments from AI shopping assistants across ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, and Gemini without a separate integration for each. PayPal describes two protocols and three paths: the Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP) for ChatGPT and OpenAI, using delegated payment tokens through Braintree; Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) with Braintree for Google AI Mode and Gemini, using a Google Pay payment handler through Braintree; and UCP with PayPal for the same Google surfaces, using a Google Pay payment handler through PayPal's Orders v2 API. Merchants use their existing Braintree or PayPal merchant account.

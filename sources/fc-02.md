# Using AI agents

**Official source** · Financial Conduct Authority · official-doc · retrieved 2026-11-25 · [open the original](https://www.fca.org.uk/consumers/using-ai-agents)

## Summary

UK Financial Conduct Authority consumer page, first published and last updated 24 November 2026. Before allowing a tool to make transactions, people should understand how it works and what protections they might have if something goes wrong, and consider how much responsibility they are comfortable giving away, including whether to consent to every transaction, set limits, or let decisions be made for them. It also warns of scams and of the risks of providing bank account details to AI agents.

## Short quotes

> You should consider how much responsibility you’re comfortable giving away. For example, whether you want to consent to every transaction made on your behalf, set limits or whether you’re content for decisions to be made for you.

> Before using a tool and allowing it to make transactions for you, you should make sure you understand how it works and what protections (if any) you might have if something goes wrong.

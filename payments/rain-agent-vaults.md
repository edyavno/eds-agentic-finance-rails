# Rain Agent Vaults

Rain Agent Vaults give an AI agent its own funded balance. The agent can spend, receive and send money from it, but only what is in the vault and only within rules its owner sets. Rain announced Agent Vaults on 2026-11-10.[^rn-01]

## How it works

- **Fund the vault.** The owner funds the vault by bank transfer, card or [stablecoins](../glossary.md#stablecoin). The agent can only pay or send what is in it.[^rn-01] Money must be in the vault before a card is issued against it.[^rn-02]
- **Pay with a scoped card.** For each purchase, Rain issues a [virtual card](../glossary.md#virtual-card) on Visa or Mastercard with the amount, merchant and expiry fixed at issuance. Rain checks those limits when the card is used, and a single-use card stops working after one approved purchase.[^rn-01]
- **Send money.** Agents can also send funds to bank accounts in more than 80 countries and send stablecoins to other wallets.[^rn-01]
- **Network agent programs.** Rain says Agent Vaults support [Visa Intelligent Commerce](visa-intelligent-commerce.md) and [Mastercard Agent Pay](mastercard-agent-pay.md),[^rn-01] and that it can generate network agentic tokens for its cards.[^rn-02] See [network token](../glossary.md#network-token).

Rain says balances are held as stablecoins in a non-custodial wallet and that users who fund from a bank account or card do not need to buy stablecoins.[^rn-01]

## What you control

- The vault owner sets the rules: how much the agent can spend, which merchants it can pay, and when a payment is allowed.[^rn-02]
- The agent cannot raise its own limit.[^rn-01]

## Who it is for

Agent Vaults are embedded in the apps of companies that build agents, under their own brand. Rain does not appear to the end user.[^rn-01] Rain says it is partnering with agent developers, marketplaces and payment platforms, and points interested companies to its sales team.[^rn-01]

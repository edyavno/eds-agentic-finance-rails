# Robinhood Agentic Credit Card

The Agentic Credit Card is a [virtual card](../glossary.md#virtual-card) inside the Robinhood Gold Card. You connect an AI agent to the Robinhood Banking [MCP](../glossary.md#mcp) server, then create the card during setup.[^rh-09] Robinhood announced it on 2026-05-27, alongside Agentic Trading.[^rh-10]

## What the agent can do

- Fetch the card's number to complete a purchase, and view the card's spending history and settings.[^rh-09]
- The MCP does not browse the internet or find things to buy. Your agent has to get to checkout on its own.[^rh-09]
- The agent can see only the agentic cards you authorize, and nothing else in your Robinhood account.[^rh-09]

## What you control

- **Approve each purchase.** You get a notice and approve the purchase in the Robinhood Banking app.[^rh-09]
- **Or a monthly limit.** If you turn off per-purchase approval, a monthly limit is required. You can change both settings later.[^rh-09]
- Robinhood says you are responsible for the purchases your agent makes.[^rh-09]

## How to connect

- Address: `https://banking-agent.robinhood.com/mcp/banking`.[^rh-09]
- Robinhood gives setup steps for Claude Code, Claude Desktop, ChatGPT (Developer Mode), Codex, Codex CLI, Cursor and OpenClaw, and says other MCP clients can connect too.[^rh-09]
- Card creation and agent sign-in work only on a desktop browser.[^rh-09]

Robinhood's trading side for agents is on the [Robinhood](../providers/robinhood.md) provider page. All programs: [Payments](README.md).

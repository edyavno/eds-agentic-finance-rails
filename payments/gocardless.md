# GoCardless agentic payments

GoCardless, a UK payments company, hosts an [MCP](../glossary.md#mcp) server that lets a business's AI agent set up a [Direct Debit](../glossary.md#direct-debit) mandate or take a one-off bank payment on a payer's behalf.[^gc-02] The rails it names are Direct Debit and [open banking](../glossary.md#open-banking).[^gc-01]

## How an agent connects

- Behind the product is a GoCardless-hosted MCP server.[^gc-02]
- GoCardless says there are two different MCPs. The GoCardless MCP is for merchants, so they can manage their own GoCardless account with an AI assistant. The commerce MCP is for payers, so they can choose and pay inside a chat.[^gc-01]
- The address of the commerce MCP is not in the pages cited here, so it is unknown.
- The merchant builds and runs the agent. It can run on a large language model such as Claude or ChatGPT, as long as the chat platform supports MCP.[^gc-01]

## Who approves what

- When the payer needs to enter an account number, name or address, a secure GoCardless form opens in the chat. The agent only gets status updates, such as "payer confirmed", rather than handling the payer's data.[^gc-02]
- GoCardless says customers remain in control of payments, with final approval.[^gc-01]
- Its Trussell post says certain forms such as Recurring Pay by Bank allow payers to set limits or revoke permissions at any time.[^gc-03]

## The Trussell payment and the FCA programme

GoCardless says it was selected for the Financial Conduct Authority's Live AI Testing Programme.[^gc-01] See [Banks and outside agents](../personal-money/banks-and-outside-agents.md) and the FCA's own [agentic AI pages](../rails/mandates-and-data-access.md#what-have-a-regulator-and-two-banks-said-about-agent-authority).

In a post last edited September 2026, GoCardless says that, working alongside that programme, it completed what it calls the UK's first agentic [account-to-account](../glossary.md#account-to-account-payment) payment: a donor set up a recurring gift to Trussell, an anti-poverty charity, through conversation with an AI agent.[^gc-03] That "first" claim is GoCardless's own.

## Who can use it

The product is in early access for UK merchants taking payments in GBP. GoCardless says to speak to its team to get it enabled on an account. A merchant needs a GoCardless account that takes BACS Direct Debit or Pay by Bank, a chat that can display the checkout, and the merchant's own agent backend.[^gc-01]

Fees are not in the pages cited here, so they are unknown.

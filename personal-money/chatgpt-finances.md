# Finances in ChatGPT

Finances in ChatGPT reads your connected accounts to show spending, bills, subscriptions and net worth and to answer questions. OpenAI says it cannot move money, pay bills, trade or change account settings.[^oa-05]

## How does it connect to your accounts?

- **Plaid for accounts.** OpenAI says you can connect financial accounts through Plaid and credit report data through Experian, as separate connections.[^oa-05] Plaid says it powers the connections, with more than 12,000 institutions, and that people can manage access through Plaid Portal.[^pb-11] Plaid is a [data aggregator](../glossary.md#data-aggregator).
- **How to start.** Open Finances from the sidebar, or say "@Finances, connect my accounts" in a chat. After you authenticate, ChatGPT syncs and categorizes your data, which can take a few minutes.[^oa-06]
- **What it can see.** OpenAI says ChatGPT can access balances, transactions, investments and liabilities, but it cannot see full account numbers or make changes to your accounts.[^oa-06]

## What it cannot do

Through Finances, ChatGPT cannot move money, pay bills, change account settings, make trades, change retirement contributions, open or close accounts or file taxes. OpenAI says these limits describe Finances and its Plaid and Experian connections, not a separate third-party app. For plugins that support transactions such as trades, it points to Connected apps in ChatGPT.[^oa-05] For paying merchants, see [ChatGPT Wallet](../payments/chatgpt-wallet.md).

## What do you control?

- You can disconnect Finances in Settings, which removes your connected financial data from ChatGPT. Disconnecting does not delete information already in your conversation history.[^oa-05]
- OpenAI says synced account data is deleted from its systems within 30 days of disconnecting.[^oa-06]
- You can view or delete financial memories from the Finances page.[^oa-05]
- With temporary chats, ChatGPT does not access your connected financial accounts or use or create memories.[^oa-05]
- Conversations with Finances follow the model training settings you choose across ChatGPT.[^oa-05]

## Availability

OpenAI launched a preview for Pro users in the US on 2026-05-15. An update dated 2026-06-25 says it reached Plus and Pro users, and one dated 2026-10-02 says it was rolling out to Free and Go users.[^oa-06] The help article now says it is available in the US to Free, Go, Plus and Pro users on web, iOS and Android.[^oa-05]

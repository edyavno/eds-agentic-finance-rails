# JPMorgan Smart Cash

Smart Cash is JPMorgan Chase's own AI tool. All facts here come from one news report, not from the bank.[^bl-01]

- JPMorgan began a targeted pilot of Smart Cash in the weeks before its 2026-10-13 earnings call.[^bl-01]

- CNBC says this looks like a defensive move against outside agents.[^bl-01] At the same time, the bank blocks outside AI agents from acting for retail customers. See [Banks and outside agents](banks-and-outside-agents.md).

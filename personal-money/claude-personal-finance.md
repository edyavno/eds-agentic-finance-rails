# Claude Personal Finance

Claude's Personal Finance feature reads balances and transactions from banks you link through Plaid. Access is read-only: it cannot move, manage or invest your money.[^cl-01][^cl-02]

## How does it connect to your accounts?

- **Plaid.** Plaid says it powers account connections for Personal Finance for US consumers. You open the Personal Finance tab, Claude prompts you to authenticate through Plaid, and Plaid shares the information needed, organizes raw transaction data and refreshes it when needed.[^pb-10] Plaid is a [data aggregator](../glossary.md#data-aggregator).
- **Your bank password stays with the bank.** Anthropic says accounts connect only once you sign in to your bank through Plaid, so Claude does not ask for or see the bank password.[^cl-02]
- **Steps.** Claude shows a "Connect Plaid to Claude" screen listing what Claude can access. In Plaid you choose your bank, sign in and pick the accounts to share. You have 10 minutes to finish, and transactions can take a few minutes to sync.[^cl-01]

## What can Claude read?

- Account names, types and last four digits, and up to 24 months of transactions with amount, date, merchant, category and your bank's description.[^cl-01]
- For investments, loans and cards, Claude reads balances and credit limits, not holdings, trades, rates or terms.[^cl-01]
- Plaid gives Claude only the last few digits of each account number.[^cl-01]

## What do you control?

- You can link up to 16 banks and unlink a bank at any time, after which Claude stops reading it. You can also disconnect Claude at my.plaid.com.[^cl-01]
- Claude reads linked accounts without asking each time unless you change the Plaid setting under Connectors. On mobile you can set Plaid to Blocked, Always allow, or Needs approval.[^cl-01]
- Claude asks before it links or unlinks a bank in a chat, and before it publishes an artifact from a chat that used your linked accounts.[^cl-01]
- Anthropic says conversations in the Personal Finance hub are not used to train its models. For anything shared outside the hub, your privacy settings apply.[^cl-01][^cl-02]

## Availability

In beta on Pro, Max and Ultra plans in the US only, on Claude, Claude Desktop, and the iOS and Android apps. It is not available on Free, Team or Enterprise plans, and Anthropic says it is rolling out gradually.[^cl-01] Personal Finance is not available in [Claude Duet](claude-duet.md).[^cl-01]

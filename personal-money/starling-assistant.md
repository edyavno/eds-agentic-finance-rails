# Starling Assistant

Starling Bank is adding payments to its in-app Starling Assistant. A customer can speak or type a prompt, the assistant sets up the payment, and the customer authorises it with a tap on the screen.[^st-01] It is a bank's own agent inside its own app, not an outside agent that logs in to a bank account.

## What can the assistant do?

- **Set up payments.** Personal and joint account holders can give voice or natural language prompts such as "Pay the plumber £200" or "Send Mum £20 for lunch". The assistant sets up the payment.[^st-01]
- **Other tasks.** Starling says the assistant already carries out tasks such as analysing direct debits and subscriptions, building personalised budgets and creating Spaces.[^st-01]
- **Business invoices.** Small business customers will soon get a similar feature: they say "Pay this invoice" and upload an image of the bill. Starling says the assistant then sets up the supplier as a payee and sets up the payment for the correct amount, and the customer approves it with a tap.[^st-01]

The payment feature is part of Starling Intelligence, which Starling calls its agentic AI platform.[^st-01]

## Who approves the payment?

The customer. Starling says the customer authorises each payment with a screen tap.[^st-01] This is the same shape as [per-order confirm](../approval-models/per-order-confirm.md), but inside the bank's own app.

## When is it available?

Starling announced it on 2026-11-13. It says the capability will reach all retail customers by the end of the year, so it is announced, not yet available to everyone. The small business version has no date.[^st-01]

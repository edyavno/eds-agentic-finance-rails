# Nous

Nous is a household AI agent in the UK. It looks after bills and household admin, and Nous says it makes decisions and takes action for a household rather than only recommending.[^ns-01][^ns-03]

## What it does with money

- It tracks bills and contracts, looks for cheaper deals and handles switching providers.[^ns-01] Nous lists energy, broadband, mobile and car insurance among the services it manages.[^ns-03]
- It finds recurring subscriptions and cancels ones you no longer want.[^ns-01]
- It reads bank data through [open banking](../glossary.md#open-banking). Nous says it never sees your bank login and that your money cannot be moved or transferred.[^ns-01]
- It works in the background and messages you on WhatsApp.[^ns-01]

## What you control

- **Savings modes.** In Manual mode, Nous flags savings and you arrange any switches. In Balanced mode, it recommends and handles switching. In Freedom mode, it makes switching decisions for you automatically.[^ns-01]
- **Permissions.** Nous works within the permissions you give it, and you can change them at any time.[^ns-01]

## Regulation

- Nous says its open banking services run through an FCA authorised and regulated open banking provider.[^ns-01] Its terms name that provider as Yapily Connect Ltd, FCA number 827001.[^ns-02]
- The company is Nous for your House Limited, registered in England.[^ns-02]
- Nous says it plans to move into more regulated activities, including broader insurance and other financial services.[^ns-03]
- Whether Nous itself holds FCA authorisation or acts as an appointed representative of an authorised firm is unknown. No cited source confirms it.

## Size and pricing

- Nous says nearly 250,000 UK households use it, a company-reported figure.[^ns-03]
- The free tier covers up to two household services. Premium costs £6.99 per household a month.[^ns-01]

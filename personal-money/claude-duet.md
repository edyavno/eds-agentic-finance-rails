# Claude Duet

Claude Duet is a proactive agent app from Anthropic, in research preview. It asks for your approval before it pays, sends a sensitive message or deletes anything, and it cannot trade securities or move money between your accounts.[^cl-03][^cl-04]

## What does it do with money?

- **Purchases.** Duet pays with a one-time card from a wallet like Link from Stripe, limited to that merchant and amount. You see the item and the total and approve the purchase in your Link app, and Anthropic says your real card number is not shared with Duet or the merchant.[^cl-03] See [Link wallet for agents](../payments/link-wallet-for-agents.md).
- **Off-limits actions.** Some actions stay off-limits even with your approval, such as trading securities, banking actions such as moving money between accounts, and accessing someone else's account.[^cl-03]
- **No Plaid account reading.** Claude's help article says Personal Finance, which reads bank accounts through Plaid, is not available in Duet.[^cl-01] See [Claude Personal Finance](claude-personal-finance.md).

## What do you control?

- **Approvals.** Before something like sending a potentially sensitive message, paying or deleting, Duet shows the action, the destination and the content, including every recipient, and waits for you to approve.[^cl-03]
- **A separate check.** An automated check built on the auto mode classifier from Claude Code runs alongside each action and assesses whether it matches your intent. It runs outside the model doing the work.[^cl-03]
- **No blanket approval for money.** Some steps are not covered by a "don't ask me again" rule however often you approve them, such as paying or sending money, deleting an order or creating an account. Routines that run while you are away have the same safeguards.[^cl-03]
- **Sensitive data.** A separate classifier checks each outgoing message. Credentials, full card and bank account numbers and government ID numbers are never shared. Health or financial details are shared only if you explicitly approve.[^cl-03]
- **Credentials.** Duet never sees your passwords, browser cookies or the sign-in tokens for your connected apps.[^cl-03]

## Availability

Anthropic opened a research preview on iOS, Android and claude.ai. It is included in Pro, Max and Ultra plans, starts with users in the US, and is offered through a waitlist.[^cl-04] The launch article is not dated on its page, so the exact launch date is unknown here.

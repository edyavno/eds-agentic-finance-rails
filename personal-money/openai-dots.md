# OpenAI dots

A dot is an always-on OpenAI agent with its own cloud computer. It can buy with a card saved on a merchant's website after you approve the purchase. OpenAI's pages do not say whether a dot can read your Finances data.[^oa-07][^oa-08]

## What can a dot do with money?

- **Buy with a saved card.** A dot can make purchases using a card you have saved on a merchant's website. These purchases require your approval, which may be given in advance when it specifically covers the purchase.[^oa-07] A dot can also pay merchants through [ChatGPT Wallet](../payments/chatgpt-wallet.md).[^oa-01]
- **Use plugins.** Plugin permissions are shared across dots, ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work and Codex, and a dot can use your existing connections within the permissions you granted.[^oa-07]
- **Sign in without exposing passwords.** For supported sign-ins, a dot pauses while you enter credentials in a secure form that sends them directly to the browser environment, without exposing them to the model.[^oa-07]

## What do you control?

- Dots start with built-in rules for when to act independently and when to ask for approval. Custom Rules let you allow specific actions, require approval or block them. Built-in safety requirements always apply.[^oa-08]
- Dots use auto-review to check actions that could affect your accounts or share information against your instructions, Custom Rules and safety requirements. Certain sensitive tasks, such as changing a password, always stay with you.[^oa-08]
- For some actions you can set rules for what your dot can share, purchase or access, and you can pause it at any time.[^oa-09]

## Availability

OpenAI announced dots on 2026-09-29, rolling out to Pro and Business Premium users in eligible markets.[^oa-08] The setup guide says Pro access is for users aged 18 and over outside the EEA, Switzerland and the UK. Enterprise, Edu and Healthcare workspaces had an admin preview from 2026-11-04 to 2026-11-18 and get dots by default from 2026-11-18 unless an admin opts out.[^oa-09]

## Unknown

- **Whether a dot can read Finances data.** [Finances in ChatGPT](chatgpt-finances.md) connects accounts through Plaid. The dots announcement, setup guide and privacy FAQ do not mention Finances or Plaid, and Plaid's posts do not list dots, so the wiki says unknown.
- Whether a dot can pay from a bank account or a wallet other than a saved merchant card or ChatGPT Wallet.
- Whether dots have an MCP server or other developer access.

# Perplexity Personal CFO

Perplexity lets people connect bank and other accounts through Plaid and ask questions about their own money. These facts come from Plaid's posts. Perplexity's own page could not be fetched, so the wiki attributes them to Plaid.[^pb-12][^pb-13]

## What does Plaid say it does?

- **In the US.** Plaid says it expanded its Perplexity integration on 2026-04-09 so users can connect more account types beyond investments. It says Perplexity Computer Pro and Max users can link credit cards, connect mortgages, auto loans and student loans, and combine accounts for a net worth view. Plaid says Perplexity then builds tools such as a net worth calculator, budget tracker and debt payoff planner.[^pb-12]
- **In Europe.** Plaid says that from 2026-11-12 people in 17 European countries, including France, Germany, Spain, Italy, Ireland, Poland and the Netherlands, can ask Perplexity questions based on their own bank account data through Personal CFO, with Plaid powering the connection.[^pb-13]
- **Aggregator.** Plaid is a [data aggregator](../glossary.md#data-aggregator). See [Plaid](plaid.md).

## What do you control?

Plaid says people choose which accounts to share and that access is read-only. In the EU, Plaid says it provides the connection as a regulated account information service through Plaid B.V.[^pb-13]

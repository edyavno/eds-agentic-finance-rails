# Gemini with Google Wallet

Google's Gemini app can answer money questions from your Google Wallet data, and from bank, card, loan and investment accounts you link to Wallet through Plaid. Google and Plaid both say its access is read-only: Gemini cannot move, manage or invest your money.[^gg-01][^pb-08] Plaid is a [data aggregator](../glossary.md#data-aggregator).

## What can Gemini do with your money data?

- **Show spending.** Google's example asks Gemini to review last quarter's spending, and says Gemini can draw charts of cash flow, asset allocation and budgets in the chat.[^gg-01]
- **Find subscriptions.** Gemini can find recurring charges across linked accounts and flag price increases.[^gg-01]
- **Plan a budget.** Gemini can look at past transactions and suggest a budget, such as for the holidays.[^gg-01]
- **Summarize.** Gemini can summarize expense activity, give budgeting tips, and summarize rewards and offers on the cards and passes in Wallet.[^gg-02]

## What can't Gemini do?

- It cannot move, manage or invest your money.[^gg-01][^pb-08]
- It cannot make transactions with the payment methods in your Google Wallet, and it cannot update or delete connected financial accounts. You do that in Google Wallet settings.[^gg-02]

## What do you control?

- You opt in. You connect Google Wallet under Connected Apps in Personal Intelligence settings, and you can turn the connection on or off at any time.[^gg-01]
- Linking financial accounts to Wallet through Plaid is optional. You can unlink them at any time in Google Wallet settings.[^gg-01][^gg-02] You can also manage your Plaid connections in Plaid Portal.[^pb-08]

## Who can use it?

Google announced it on 2026-11-13 for all Gemini app users in the US, on web and mobile (Android and iOS).[^gg-01] Google's help page adds that you must be 18 or over and in the US, and sign in with a personal Google Account rather than a work or school one. Gemini needs History turned on, and the connection is English only, in the Gemini mobile app and the web app, and not in Gems or Gemini Live. Google says it is making the feature available gradually, so it might not be available to you yet.[^gg-02]

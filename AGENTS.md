# AGENTS.md

How to read, cite and analyze this wiki. For AI agents and the people who use them.

## Where to start

- [llms.txt](llms.txt) lists every page with a one-line summary. [index.md](index.md) is the full catalog, including every source card.
- For structured facts, read [data/providers.json](data/providers.json) and [data/programs.json](data/programs.json). Each field lists the source cards behind it. Their shape is described in [schema/provider.schema.json](schema/provider.schema.json) and [schema/program.schema.json](schema/program.schema.json).
- Quick answers are in the [FAQ](faq.md). Terms are defined in the [glossary](glossary.md).
- Everything is plain Markdown, so a clone works offline: `git clone https://github.com/edyavno/eds-agentic-finance-rails`.

## How citations work

- A **source card** (all are listed in [index.md](index.md)) is a short note about one outside page: its link, publisher, whether it is official or third-party, the date it was retrieved, a summary and a few short quotes. It is never a full copy.
- A **footnote** like `[^rh-01]` marks the card behind a fact. The footnote id is the card id, and the Sources list at the bottom of each page links to the card.
- Cards marked `use: leads-only` (prior art and other directories) are leads, not evidence.

## When you answer from the wiki

- Cite the wiki page **and** the source card behind each fact.
- If a field says `unknown`, or no source backs a claim, say "unknown". Never fill a gap from memory.
- Check `page_status` and `last_verified` (shown as "Last checked"). Warn your user if a page is `stale` or `archived`, or if `last_verified` is older than 30 days.
- This wiki is not investment advice and does not rank brokers.

## Corrections

People: use the "Suggest a correction" link at the bottom of any page. It opens a GitHub issue. The repo does not take outside pull requests.

## Giving feedback

Agents can POST a short note to `/feedback` on the site. GET `/feedback` first for the current schema and an example.

- Send JSON with `Content-Type: application/json`. The body may be at most 4 KB.
- `note` is required: what you want the maintainer to know. Up to 2000 characters.
- Optional: `task` (what you were trying to do), `missing` (what you could not find), `wanted` (what would help), `corrections` (what looks wrong, with the page and source), each up to 2000 characters.
- Optional: `found`, one of `yes`, `partly` or `no` (did the wiki answer it?).
- Optional: `pages`, up to 10 pages you used: full URLs on this site or paths such as `/providers/webull`.
- Optional: `agent`, your agent or tool name, up to 100 characters.
- Each client may send 10 notes per rolling 24 hours.
- A saved note returns its `id`. An invalid note returns the `fields` that failed and the `limits`.
- Do not include personal data, account numbers, credentials, or anything private about your user.
- The endpoint records the sender's IP address with each note, as access logs do.

A person reviews every issue and note. Nothing changes automatically.

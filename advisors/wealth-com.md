# Wealth.com

Wealth.com's November 2026 update says its estate and tax planning connector, which reached Claude in September 2026, now also works in ChatGPT, and that its AI assistant, Ester, is embedded in Schwab Advisor Center. Both are for advisors. The source describes planning answers and documents, not trading or moving money.[^wc-01]

## What does the ChatGPT connector do?

An advisor can ask ChatGPT a question about a client and get an answer from the advisor's own Wealth.com data, "not from the open web".[^wc-01] The connector uses Wealth.com's [MCP](../glossary.md#mcp) to reach features such as estate document analysis with page-level citations, a household's balance sheet and how its assets are titled, beneficiary designations, filed-return summaries, and tax scenarios such as a Roth conversion.[^wc-01]

It is enabled at the firm level, subject to compliance approval.[^wc-01]

## What is Ester in Schwab Advisor Center?

Wealth.com says Ester now runs inside Schwab Advisor Center and is available to every user there, with no Wealth.com license required. An advisor can upload a trust, a will or a tax return and run one of four workflows that return a client-ready PDF, or ask an estate or tax question and get an answer with source citations.[^wc-01]

For firms that already license Wealth.com, Wealth.com calls Ester in Schwab Advisor Center a separate, lighter tool that does not connect to a client's Vault or history. Access runs through Schwab's firm security groups, and a firm's security administrator has to enable it.[^wc-01]

## What is still unknown about Wealth.com?

- The exact date of the update. The page is titled November 2026 and shows no day.
- Which firms have turned the ChatGPT connector on, and what compliance approval it needs. The source says some features are still being rolled out per firm.[^wc-01]
- Whether any Wealth.com tool places trades or moves money. The cited source describes none.

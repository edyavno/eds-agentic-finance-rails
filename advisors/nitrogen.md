# Nitrogen

Nitrogen offers advisor agents through an official MCP server and its Nucleus agentic AI engine, and its official agents page says every action still requires the advisor's approval.[^ni-01][^ni-02]

Nitrogen documents advisor agents on its official agents page and in a Fall 2026 product announcement distributed on GlobeNewswire (Source: Nitrogen).[^ni-01][^ni-02]

## Official agents page

The official page gives the MCP server URL `https://api2.riskalyze.com/mcp`.[^ni-01]

It says Claude is "Now available in the Connectors Directory." For ChatGPT, it says a "Custom App now available via Developer mode. Nitrogen Plugin coming soon to the ChatGPT Plugins Directory." For Microsoft 365 Copilot, the page says it is "available by request."[^ni-01]

On approval, the page says: "Every action still requires your approval, and client data is never used to train a model." In a FAQ it answers "Can agents do any work on the advisor’s behalf without advisor approval?" with: "No. Anything that touches client data still requires a human “yes.”"[^ni-01]

The page also describes Nucleus as now live at no additional cost, with a footnote that Nucleus "is not available in some legacy plans, and is still in the approval process at some broker dealers."[^ni-01]

## Fall 2026 announcement

The November 10, 2026 announcement says Nitrogen announced its Fall 2026 product release, "bringing its connected suite of advisor products into Claude and ChatGPT while expanding the capabilities of Nucleus™, its agentic AI engine." It also introduced integrations with Contio and Zocks.[^ni-02]

It says advisors can access Nitrogen through Claude and ChatGPT, use Nucleus in Slack and Microsoft Teams, and connect through Microsoft Copilot, "at no additional cost for advisors with Nucleus access." It also says "Nucleus can now plan and execute multi-step workflows across products."[^ni-02]

An "About Nitrogen" section in that announcement includes company-reported figures: "Trusted by tens of thousands of advisors, backed by an industry-leading Net Promoter Score of 71, and ISO 42001 certified."[^ni-02]
